**Madhu Guru, Sr Director AI @Meta**

Former Gemini and Veo lead Madhu Guru pushes back on a popular narrative: open-weight models didn't *cause* the drop in AI pricing—that trend was already underway. The real drivers have been: (1) model builders distilling their best models into smaller, cheaper-to-run versions, (2) infrastructure efficiency improvements, and (3) competition between providers. "You can see this pattern with every model company: Version x of the mid-size model = intelligence of version x-1 of the large model. Same intelligence for less $." Open weights are a tailwind, not the origin.
https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2108618266776387886

前 Gemini 和 Veo 负责人 Madhu Guru 反驳了一种流行叙事：开源权重模型并没有*导致* AI 定价下降——这个趋势在此之前就已经出现。真正的驱动力是：(1) 模型公司将最优秀的大模型蒸馏成更小、推理成本更低的版本；(2) 基础设施效率提升；(3) 模型供应商之间的竞争。"你能在每家模型公司看到这种模式：中等规模模型的第 x 版本 = 大模型第 x-1 版本的智能水平。同等智能，价格更低。"开源权重是顺风，但不是起点。

## OFFICIAL BLOGS

**Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products**

Anthropic Engineering details their evolving approach to "blast radius" management as Claude agents take on increasingly high-stakes access. A year ago, granting Claude access sufficient to take down an internal Anthropic service would have been unthinkable—today it's routine. The post frames risk in two components: the probability of failure (which training and safeguards steadily reduce) and the blast radius if failure does occur (which only grows as capabilities expand).

Their answer is containment: using environment controls, human-in-the-loop supervision, and automated mechanisms to cap how much damage any autonomous action can do. The post acknowledges that Claude Mythos Preview—described as having an unacceptably high blast radius—was held back from public release in April 2026 despite being a capable model. Broader release of similar models is expected "as defenders harden critical systems and safeguards mature."

> "As agents become capable of doing work that once required a person or even a team, the cost of not deploying grows large enough that the risk-reward calculation tips heavily toward adoption, as long as products can be made safe."

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

Anthropic Engineering 详细介绍了随着 Claude agent 获得越来越高权限访问，他们不断演进的"爆炸半径"管控方法。一年前，授予 Claude 足以影响 Anthropic 内部服务的访问权限还是不可想象的——今天这已经是常规操作。文章将风险拆分为两个维度：失败的概率（通过训练和安全措施持续降低）和失败发生时的爆炸半径（随着能力扩展只会增加）。

他们的答案是"遏制"：通过环境控制、人工监督循环和自动化机制来限制任何自主行动可能造成的破坏上限。文章承认，Claude Mythos Preview——一个被认为爆炸半径过高的模型——尽管能力出众，仍在 2026 年 4 月被暂缓公开发布。随着"防御者强化关键系统、安全措施成熟"，类似能力水平的模型预计将更广泛地发布。

> "随着 agent 能够完成曾经需要一个人甚至一个团队才能完成的工作，不部署的代价变得足够大，只要产品可以做到安全，风险收益的计算就会强烈倾向于采用。"

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

**Anthropic Engineering: An update on recent Claude Code quality reports**

Anthropic published a postmortem on three separate issues that degraded Claude Code quality between March and April 2026. All three have been resolved as of April 20 (v2.1.116):

1. **Default reasoning effort downgrade**: On March 4, Claude Code's default reasoning was silently switched from "high" to "medium" to reduce UI freezes. Users noticed the intelligence drop and it was reverted on April 7.

2. **Context forgetting bug**: A March 26 change designed to clear idle session context (after an hour of inactivity) had a bug causing it to clear context every single turn—making Claude appear forgetful and repetitive. Fixed April 10.

3. A third issue also affected the Claude Agent SDK and Claude Cowork, now resolved as of April 20.

The API was not impacted. The postmortem commits to changes in how quality regressions are caught and communicated going forward.

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/april-23-postmortem

Anthropic 发布了一份事后分析，详述了 2026 年 3 月至 4 月间导致 Claude Code 质量下降的三个独立问题。所有问题已于 4 月 20 日（v2.1.116）全部修复：

1. **默认推理努力降级**：3 月 4 日，为减少 UI 卡顿，Claude Code 的默认推理等级从"高"悄悄切换为"中"。用户察觉到了智能水平下降，4 月 7 日已回滚。

2. **上下文遗忘 bug**：3 月 26 日的一个变更旨在清除空闲会话上下文（闲置超过一小时后），但存在 bug，导致每一轮对话都会清除上下文——让 Claude 看起来很健忘、重复。4 月 10 日已修复。

3. 还有第三个问题也影响了 Claude Agent SDK 和 Claude Cowork，已于 4 月 20 日修复。

API 未受影响。此次事后分析还承诺了未来如何更好地捕捉和沟通质量回退问题的改进措施。

**Anthropic Engineering: Scaling Managed Agents — Decoupling the brain from the hands**

Anthropic's engineering blog introduces Claude Managed Agents: a hosted service that runs long-horizon agents on your behalf through stable, versioned interfaces. The core insight: harnesses (the scaffolding around Claude) encode assumptions about what Claude *can't* do—and those assumptions go stale as models improve. Example: Claude Sonnet 4.5 would abandon tasks as it sensed its context limit approaching ("context anxiety"). They built a context reset mechanism to compensate. When Claude Opus 4.5 arrived, the anxiety was gone—making the reset dead weight.

Rather than keep patching harnesses, Anthropic built Managed Agents as stable abstractions (inspired by OS design: `process`, `file`, `read()`) that decouple the *brain* (the model) from the *hands* (the execution environment). The model improves underneath; the interface stays constant.

> "Harnesses encode assumptions about what Claude can't do on its own. However, those assumptions need to be frequently questioned because they can go stale as models improve."

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents

Anthropic 工程博客介绍了 Claude Managed Agents：一项托管服务，通过稳定的、版本化的接口代表用户运行长时程 agent。核心洞见在于：harness（Claude 外部的脚手架）编码了 Claude *不能*做什么的假设——而随着模型进步，这些假设会过时。例如：Claude Sonnet 4.5 在感知到上下文限制临近时会提前放弃任务（"上下文焦虑"）。他们在 harness 中构建了上下文重置机制来弥补。当 Claude Opus 4.5 上线后，这种焦虑消失了——重置机制反而成了死重。

与其持续打补丁，Anthropic 借鉴操作系统设计（`process`、`file`、`read()`），将 Managed Agents 构建为稳定的抽象层，将*大脑*（模型）与*双手*（执行环境）解耦。模型在底层持续进步；接口保持不变。

> "Harness 编码了 Claude 自身不能做什么的假设。然而，这些假设需要被频繁质疑，因为随着模型进步，它们可能已经过时。"

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents

## PODCASTS

**No Priors: "Beam: The Great American Open Model" — Misha Laskin, Co-Founder & CEO of Reflection AI**

**The Takeaway:** Reflection AI is building the first serious American open-weight frontier model—not just to compete with closed labs, but because they believe open models are essential for safety: "With enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow as well."

Misha Laskin founded Reflection AI after leaving Google DeepMind (where he worked on the Gemini and Veo teams with his co-founder Janus, who was a key contributor to AlphaGo). His bet two years ago: reinforcement learning would become the dominant training paradigm, open-weight models from China were ahead of Western alternatives, and there was both a geopolitical and research argument for building American open models end-to-end. It took a lab of 300 people and roughly two and a half years to reach their first model launch.

Their first model, *Beam*, is a 500 billion total parameter (23B active) mixture-of-experts model. Pretraining consumed 6,000 GB300s for about 12 days; reinforcement learning consumed even more. The striking observation about RL training: the loss curves never flatten. "If you look at our plots, they just keep going up. It's just a matter of compute basically in terms of scaling it further." He argues the field has crossed a fundamental threshold—RL systems are now general enough that training is less a research problem and more an engineering/economics decision about how much compute to invest.

> "We as a field are there where you have these RL systems that are very general that don't really stop improving... it's just an economics decision of how much money do I put into X to get some kind of improvement out of it, which is incredible. This was not true two years ago."

On open source and safety, Laskin inverts the standard safety argument: "When you remove cyber offensive capabilities, you also remove cyber defensive capabilities... A very powerful closed model went and hacked into another company, and the only way that company could remediate itself was by using open models to protect itself." He invokes Linus's Law: with enough eyeballs, bugs become shallow—and believes the same applies to AI safety vulnerabilities.

https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast
