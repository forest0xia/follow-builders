### Latent Space — Designing for Agents: Zeno Rocha, Resend

**The Takeaway:** Winning in a commodity infrastructure market isn't about out-engineering competitors — it's about out-caring them on the developer experience, then making sure the AI models recommend you.

Zeno Rocha came from the front-end world — the VS Code theme guy, top-20 most active GitHub user in 2022, with no email or infrastructure background whatsoever. That naivety turned out to be his edge. Where incumbents like SendGrid and Mailchimp had built processes optimized for enterprise compliance (think: a 2-day approval process before you can send a single email), Rocha asked why any of that friction had to exist on the customer side. His answer: remove all signup friction, trust users by default, and move the burden of abuse detection to Resend's internal systems.

Three years in, the results are hard to argue with. Resend now powers 4.3 million users and 120,000 paid customers — including Warner Brothers and HBO — and recently surpassed SendGrid on downloads. "It's insane — now we're more than double."

The more counterintuitive revelation is how Resend thinks about email deliverability. It turns out "getting to the inbox" at scale is an art form: you can't just blast 100,000 emails at Gmail. You put the most engaged audience members first in the queue, batch carefully across IP pools, and design sending patterns that look nothing like a phishing campaign. "You need to do exactly what an abuser wouldn't do." Resend auto-generates plain-text versions of every email (extracted from HTML) because spam filters read it more easily — and that alone boosted deliverability significantly.

What's next is an infrastructure play. Rocha hinted at a major announcement coming soon: "We've been working for months now, going down to the metal." And the reason AI agents reflexively recommend Resend even when users haven't asked? He spent years making sure the product showed up everywhere developers look. It was deliberate from day one.

> "Every generation, you have those tools that define that moment. We remember the Heroku days, the Netlify days. There's room for a new generation, and this new generation is not gonna be distributed in the same places it was before."

**Latent Space 播客 — 为 Agent 而设计：Zeno Rocha，Resend**

**核心洞察：** 在同质化基础设施市场中取胜，靠的不是比竞争对手技术更强——而是对开发者体验的投入程度超越所有人，再让 AI 模型自然而然地推荐你。

Zeno Rocha 来自前端世界——那个做 VS Code 主题的人，2022 年 GitHub 最活跃用户前 20，完全没有电子邮件或基础设施背景。这种"天真"反而成了他的优势。在 SendGrid、Mailchimp 等老牌玩家用企业级流程（想象一下：发送第一封邮件前需要经历两天人工审核）优化合规性的地方，Rocha 问的问题是：为什么这些摩擦必须由客户来承担？他的答案是：去除所有注册障碍，默认信任用户，把反滥用检测的负担转移到 Resend 内部系统。

三年后，结果不言而喻。Resend 现已为 430 万用户和 12 万付费客户提供服务——包括华纳兄弟和 HBO——并且在下载量上最近超越了 SendGrid。"这太疯狂了——现在我们的量是他们的两倍多。"

更反直觉的揭示，是 Resend 对邮件送达率的思考。事实证明，大规模"进入收件箱"是一门艺术：你不能直接向 Gmail 发送 10 万封邮件。你要把参与度最高的用户排在队列前面，在 IP 池之间谨慎分批，设计出与网络钓鱼攻击完全不同的发送模式。"你需要做的，正是滥用者不会做的事。" Resend 为每封邮件自动生成纯文本版本（从 HTML 中提取），因为垃圾邮件过滤器读取纯文本更容易——仅此一项就显著提升了送达率。

接下来是一个基础设施层面的大动作。Rocha 暗示即将有重大公告："我们已经为此工作了数月，正在深入底层。" 至于为什么 AI agent 在用户没有要求的情况下也会推荐 Resend？他花了数年时间确保产品出现在每一个开发者会看的地方。这是从第一天起就刻意为之的。

> "每个时代，都有定义那个时代的工具。我们记得 Heroku 时代、Netlify 时代。新一代有它的空间，只是这一代不会通过同样的渠道传播。"
