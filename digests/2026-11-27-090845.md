**Latent Space: The Revolution vs The Renaissance — Thariq Shihipar, OpenAI**

The Takeaway: AI's next era isn't about removing the human from the loop — it's about redesigning the loop so humans can actually shape what gets built.

Thariq Shihipar (recently joining OpenAI from Anthropic, closing keynote at the AI Engineer Code Summit) introduced a framework that reframes the dominant narrative around AI and software. The "revolution" is the efficiency story: AI makes software cheaper, more reliable, easier to produce at scale — and also produces a lot of slop. But there's a companion story — the "renaissance" — where AI makes it affordable to build something personal, to raise the level of your craft, to spend time refining how software actually feels.

His critique of current vibe coding: the experience is disorienting because "I didn't even know this character did this in this circumstance." His solution was to create a `/human` scripting layer — a folder with linting rules optimized for human readability (dense comments, limited line length, full variable names), so the actual code becomes legible without being documentation. It's a spec-like layer that the human controls while the model handles the underlying primitives.

One sharp observation on AI-generated defaults: "Most generated websites look the same. You ask it to make a game, it will by default add like if you hold Shift, you'll boost." The revolution delivers standard quality. The renaissance is what happens when you iterate beyond the default.

He made a broader point using game design as a metaphor: a perfectly balanced game is boring. Rock, Paper, Scissors is perfectly balanced — and boring. Good systems have variance, imbalance, and an explorable meta. The same logic may apply to how AI research allocates compute.

On the future of knowledge work: Shihipar sees RSI converting technically demanding fields (biology, robotics, interpretability) into knowledge work — where the experiments are automated, and humans focus on deciding which experiments to run and allocating resources. His hope is a world where even running a restaurant becomes "white collar" in this sense.

**Latent Space：革命与文艺复兴 — Thariq Shihipar，OpenAI**

核心洞见：AI 的下一个时代不是把人类从循环中移除，而是重新设计这个循环，让人类能真正塑造所构建的东西。

Thariq Shihipar（刚从 Anthropic 加入 OpenAI，AI Engineer Code Summit 闭幕主旨演讲人）提出了一个新框架，挑战关于 AI 与软件的主流叙事。"革命"是效率故事：AI 让软件更便宜、更可靠、更容易规模化生产——但也制造了大量"垃圾"。但还有另一个故事——"文艺复兴"——AI 让你有能力构建真正个性化的东西，提升你的工艺水准，花时间打磨软件的"感觉"。

他对当前"氛围编程"的批评：这种体验令人迷失方向，因为"我甚至不知道这个角色在这种情况下会这样做"。他的解决方案是创建一个 `/human` 脚本层——一个有着特殊 lint 规则的目录，优化为人类可读性（高密度注释、有限行长、完整变量名），让实际代码无需文档就能被人理解。这是一个人类掌控的规格层，底层原语由模型负责实现。

他对 AI 生成默认值的一个敏锐观察："大多数 AI 生成的网站看起来都一样。你让它做个游戏，它默认会加：按住 Shift 加速。" 革命带来标准质量。文艺复兴是在默认值之上不断迭代的结果。

他借用游戏设计作为更广泛的隐喻：一款完美平衡的游戏是无聊的。石头剪刀布完美平衡——也很无聊。好的系统需要方差、失衡和可探索的元游戏。同样的逻辑或许适用于 AI 研究如何分配算力。

关于知识工作的未来：Shihipar 认为 RSI 将把生物学、机器人学、可解释性等技术密集型领域转化为知识工作——实验自动化完成，人类专注于决定做哪些实验和分配资源。他的愿景是：即使是经营餐厅也能以这种意义上变成"白领"工作的世界。
