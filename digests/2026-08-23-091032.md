**Madhu Guru (Sr. Director of AI at Meta, ex-Google)** continues his "How to Build Great Evals" series with Part 6, focused on hill climbing. His framing: hill climbing is just picking a dimension that matters and optimizing for it — whether that's improving quality on high-value user journeys, expanding to adjacent use cases, or reducing cost and latency. The practical work comes down to better harnesses and model selection through prompt engineering, context engineering, memory, post-training, or deterministic code. He uses tool calling failures as a concrete example: if your product stuffs 20 tools into context when each task only needs 3–5, the fix is targeted context engineering, not model-swapping. His broader advice — launch with the best model first to nail quality, then hill climb toward a cheaper, faster model once users love the experience.

**Madhu Guru（Meta AI 高级总监，前 Google）**继续更新《如何构建优秀 Evals》系列，第 6 篇聚焦"爬坡优化"（hill climbing）。他的核心框架：爬坡不过是选定一个重要维度并持续优化——可以是提升高价值用户旅程的质量、拓展相邻用例，也可以是降低成本和延迟。实际工作落脚在更好的测试框架与模型选型，手段包括 prompt 工程、上下文工程、记忆机制、后训练或传统确定性代码。他以工具调用失败为具体示例：如果你的产品在上下文中塞入 20 个工具，而每个任务实际只需要 3-5 个，解法是精准的上下文工程，而非换模型。他的大原则：先用最好的模型做出用户喜爱的体验，再逐步爬坡到更便宜、更快的模型。

**AI & I by Every — "Microsoft's Vision for an Internet Made for Agents With CTO Kevin Scott (Best of the Pod)"**

**The Takeaway:** The next great unlock for AI agents isn't better models — it's building the internet-like infrastructure that lets those models actually take action in the world.

Microsoft CTO Kevin Scott has been programming for 41 years and has spent the last two building a case that the industry is sitting on a "capability overhang": models are now more capable than the products built on top of them. The gap to close isn't reasoning — it's infrastructure.

Scott draws a direct parallel between the emerging agentic web and the early internet. HTTP and HTML made the web composable; MCP and NL web are attempting to do the same for agents. His push inside Microsoft: get all internal systems speaking a standard protocol to agents, so you don't end up "shipping your org chart" — a reference to Conway's Law, where an organization's structure gets baked into its software architecture.

On the question of open vs. closed ecosystems, Scott pushes back on the false dichotomy. He's genuinely excited about permissionless innovation — the ability for someone with an idea to reach people who need it without going through gatekeepers. But he also sees a path to real security in open systems, potentially powered by AI agents that know your personal risk profile and monitor on your behalf.

On the future of software engineering and the craft debate — whether using AI agents is "real" coding — Scott draws on his parallel life as a woodworker. The debate about hand tools vs. power tools vs. CNC tools is decades old. His advice: "Be curious. Try stuff. And if it works for you, use it. And if it doesn't, don't."

His prediction for the next year: expect a shift from synchronous to asynchronous agent interaction. Right now people prompt and wait. Soon agents will go off, make dozens of calls, integrate results, and come back to you much later with a completed chunk of work — more like delegating to a capable colleague than querying a chatbot.

**AI & I by Every — "Microsoft's Vision for an Internet Made for Agents With CTO Kevin Scott (Best of the Pod)"**（中文摘要）

**核心洞见：** AI agent 的下一个重大突破不在于更好的模型，而在于构建一套类似互联网的基础设施，让这些模型真正能够在世界中采取行动。

微软 CTO Kevin Scott 编程 41 年，过去两年一直在论证一个观点：整个行业正面临"能力过剩悬挂"——模型的能力已经超越了建立在它们之上的产品。需要填补的差距不是推理能力，而是基础设施。

Scott 将新兴的 agentic web 与早期互联网类比：HTTP 和 HTML 让 web 变得可组合；MCP 和 NL web 正试图为 agent 实现同样的目标。他在微软内部力推：让所有内部系统使用统一协议与 agent 通信，避免"把组织架构编译进产品"——这是对 Conway 定律的引用，意指组织结构会渗透进软件架构。

在开放与封闭生态的问题上，Scott 拒绝将其视为非此即彼的选择。他真心欣赏无需许可的创新——让有想法的人不经过守门人就能触达需要的用户。但他也认为开放系统完全可以实现真正的安全，甚至可以借助了解你个人风险偏好、主动监控的 AI agent 来实现。

对于软件工程的工艺争论——用 AI agent 写代码算不算"真正的"编程——Scott 以他的木工经历作答。手工工具、电动工具还是 CNC 数控工具之争已经延续了几十年。他的建议：保持好奇心，去尝试，管用就用，不管用就算了。

他对未来一年的预测：agent 交互将从同步转向异步。现在人们发送 prompt 然后等待结果；不久的将来，agent 将独立去做大量调用、整合信息，在一段时间后带着完整的成果回来——更像是把任务委托给一个靠谱的同事，而不是查询一个聊天机器人。
