**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck**
*Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer | Renen Hallak, VAST Data*

**The Takeaway:** The most critical — and most overlooked — layer of the AI stack is the software infrastructure that sits between raw compute and models, and VAST Data has quietly built a $30B business there by becoming the operating system for the AI era.

Renen Hallak, founder and CEO of VAST Data, has a front-row seat to the scale of AI infrastructure demand that most people never hear about. A single customer recently came back and revised their storage estimate from 500 petabytes over three years to 500 petabytes plus two extra exabytes — all in one conversation. That's the velocity Hallak is operating in.

His core argument is that every layer of the old technology stack — power, hardware, networking, storage, databases — is being rebuilt from scratch. The new stack needs 500-kilowatt racks instead of 10-kilowatt ones, ultra-fast SSDs instead of spinning drives, and storage systems that can handle unstructured data (images, video, audio, natural language) at a scale that makes traditional databases irrelevant. VAST Data sits in that middle layer, which Hallak calls "the operating system for the AI era."

The most contrarian thesis in the conversation is about enterprise AI ownership. Hallak argues that organizations won't just use AI from the big labs forever — they'll increasingly need to own their own models, weights, and agents, because their proprietary processes and IP are too valuable to hand off to external providers. "You don't want to because you don't want to expose your proprietary information, your processes, your secret sauce out to somebody else such that they can take it and run with it."

His analogy for the path forward: in the 1970s you needed a PhD to operate a computer. Today everyone can. That transition happened because software infrastructure — the operating system — made it accessible. The same abstraction layer is being built for AI now, and whoever builds it controls the next decade of enterprise compute.

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck**
*Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer | Renen Hallak, VAST Data*

**核心洞察：** AI 技术栈中最关键、也最容易被忽视的一层，是位于原始算力和模型之间的软件基础设施——而 VAST Data 正是凭借成为 AI 时代"操作系统"，悄然建立起一家 300 亿美元的公司。

VAST Data 创始人兼 CEO Renen Hallak，是少数能亲历 AI 基础设施需求规模的人之一。他分享了一个令人震惊的细节：一位客户最初预估未来三年需要 500 PB 存储，几周后再次找来，说还需要额外增加两个 EB（1 EB = 1000 PB）。这就是 Hallak 日常运营的速度。

他的核心论点是：旧技术栈的每一层——电力、硬件、网络、存储、数据库——都正在被从头重建。新栈需要 500 千瓦级机架而非 10 千瓦，需要超高速 SSD 而非机械硬盘，还需要能处理非结构化数据（图像、视频、音频、自然语言）的存储系统，其规模让传统数据库完全失效。VAST Data 正是处于这个中间层，Hallak 将其称为"AI 时代的操作系统"。

对话中最反直觉的论断涉及企业 AI 自主权。Hallak 认为，企业不会永远只依赖大模型公司的服务——他们最终需要拥有自己的模型、权重和 agent，因为其专有流程和知识产权太宝贵，不能交由外部供应商掌控。"你不会希望把你的专有信息、流程、核心秘密暴露给别人，让他们拿去随意使用。"

他用一个类比描述未来的路径：上世纪 70 年代，操作计算机需要计算机科学博士学位。今天人人都会用。这个转变发生的原因，是操作系统让技术变得触手可及。同样的抽象层如今正在为 AI 时代构建，谁建成了它，谁就掌控了未来十年的企业计算。
