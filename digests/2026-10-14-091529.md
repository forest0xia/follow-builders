### Anthropic Engineering: Redesigning source control at Anthropic

At Anthropic, Claude now writes code so prolifically that the company went from merging 2,500 code changes per day in May 2026 to over 30,000 per day by September — a 12x increase in four months. The bottleneck is no longer engineers writing code; it's the infrastructure built for human-scale development. To handle the load, Anthropic built Scion, an in-house version control system that replaces Git, alongside a new code review surface called Antique, code search, and a rich IAM system — all within five months.

在 Anthropic，Claude 写代码的速度已经变得如此之快，公司从 2026 年 5 月每天合并 2,500 次代码变更，到 9 月已超过 30,000 次——四个月内增长了 12 倍。现在的瓶颈不再是工程师写代码，而是为人类规模开发而建造的基础设施。为应对这一挑战，Anthropic 在五个月内自研了一套名为 Scion 的版本控制系统来替代 Git，同时还构建了名为 Antique 的代码审查界面、代码搜索以及完整的 IAM 系统。

The core insight behind Scion: Git is designed for decentralized, offline use — every clone is a self-contained repository. But Anthropic's clients are overwhelmingly agents and CI jobs that are always online, so Git's offline-first design becomes a liability at scale. The main branch history alone is nearly 100 GB; a compressed working copy is 25 GB; and they perform around 25 million code checkouts per day just for CI. Scion is designed around always-online clients, with the server handling commits, merges, rebases, history, and blame — while the client maintains only a minimal local cache. Every component is horizontally scalable; there's no fully materialized copy of the repo anywhere, not even on the server.

Scion 背后的核心洞察：Git 是为去中心化、离线使用而设计的——每次克隆都是一个完整的独立仓库。但 Anthropic 的客户端绝大多数是始终在线的 agent 和 CI 任务，因此 Git 的离线优先设计在规模化时反而成了负担。仅主分支的历史记录就接近 100 GB，压缩后的工作副本有 25 GB，仅 CI 每天就要执行约 2,500 万次代码检出。Scion 基于始终在线的客户端设计，由服务器处理提交、合并、变基、历史和追溯，而客户端只维护一个最小的本地缓存。每个组件都可以水平扩展；甚至连服务器上都没有任何完整实体化的仓库副本。

Practical implications: Scion exposes all of its source control queries via API, so CI jobs can often skip local checkouts entirely. Commits are cheap, incremental, and lock-free — their cost scales with the size of the change, not the repo size. Anthropic notes that while most organizations won't face these pressures immediately, AI coding tools are rapidly pushing the industry toward this inflection point.

实际意义：Scion 通过 API 暴露所有源代码控制查询，因此 CI 任务通常可以完全跳过本地检出。提交操作代价低廉、增量式、无锁——其成本与变更大小成比例，而不是与整个仓库大小挂钩。Anthropic 指出，尽管大多数组织目前不会立刻面临这些压力，但 AI 编程工具正在迅速推动整个行业走向这一拐点。

### Training Data: David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence — A Superlearner for the Age of Experience

**The Takeaway:** AlphaGo creator David Silver believes the path to superintelligence runs through systems that learn entirely from experience — not through scaling LLMs — and he left DeepMind to bet his career on it.

**核心观点：** AlphaGo 的创造者 David Silver 相信，通往超级智能的道路在于完全从经验中学习的系统——而非扩展 LLM——他为此离开 DeepMind，将职业生涯押注于此。

David Silver is best known for leading the AlphaGo project, whose 2009 PhD on reinforcement learning applied to Go demonstrated for the first time that machine intelligence could surpass humans in a complex domain. He co-founded Ineffable Intelligence with Lasse Espeholt, formerly of Google DeepMind, with a singular conviction: the approach that got AI to where it is today — pre-training on human-generated data — is not sufficient for reaching superintelligence. Silver argues that true superintelligence requires a system that can discover entirely new knowledge on its own, just as human civilization collectively built science, mathematics, and language from scratch. That capability, he believes, has not yet been cracked.

David Silver 因领导 AlphaGo 项目而闻名于世，他 2009 年关于强化学习应用于围棋的博士研究首次证明了机器智能可以在复杂领域超越人类。他与前 Google DeepMind 研究员 Lasse Espeholt 共同创立了 Ineffable Intelligence，带着一个坚定的信念：让 AI 走到今天这一步的方法——在人类生成数据上做预训练——不足以达到超级智能。Silver 认为，真正的超级智能需要一个能够完全自主发现全新知识的系统，就像人类文明从零开始集体建立起科学、数学和语言一样。他相信，这种能力至今尚未被攻克。

"Collectively, we somehow started out knowing nothing at all and somehow discovered everything that humans know today — this process of collective discovery of languages and technology and science and mathematics and all the rest of it. Everything we learned, we discovered it for ourselves. And that's an incredible thing that no one has cracked yet in AI."

"从集体角度看，我们曾经对一切一无所知，却不知如何发现了人类今天所掌握的一切——这是一个关于语言、技术、科学、数学等等所有知识的集体发现过程。我们所学到的一切，都是自己探索出来的。而这在 AI 领域至今无人攻克，这是一件了不起的事。"

The question of why to leave DeepMind — where Silver had both resources and influence — gets a surprisingly direct answer. With hundreds of thousands of researchers worldwide doubling down on LLMs and the pre-training paradigm, Silver concluded that being one more voice in that chorus was a poor use of his particular edge. There's a lamppost nobody is looking under: learning from experience, without human data as the foundation. If that approach works, it changes everything. Lasse Espeholt, who built expertise scaling reinforcement learning on GPUs during his years at DeepMind and Google Brain, joined as co-founder. The company's name, "Ineffable," captures the ambition: the knowledge superintelligence will one day discover may be so profound it transcends human language — which, fittingly, is also an approach that doesn't route through human language at all.

被问及为何离开资源充裕、影响力深远的 DeepMind，Silver 给出了一个出人意料的直接回答。全球数以十万计的研究者都在加倍押注 LLM 和预训练范式，他由此得出结论：在那个合唱中再多一个声音，是对自身独特优势的浪费。有一盏无人关注的路灯：不依赖人类数据、完全从经验中学习。如果这条路走得通，将改变一切。在 DeepMind 和 Google Brain 积累了多年 GPU 强化学习扩展经验的 Lasse Espeholt 以联合创始人身份加入。公司名称"Ineffable（难以言说）"承载着这份雄心：超级智能有朝一日发现的知识，可能深刻到超越人类语言的表达——而这恰好也是一种完全不经由人类语言的研究路径。
