**Andrej Karpathy**

Karpathy dropped one of his most widely-shared posts this cycle: a detailed breakdown of the AI capability funnel. He estimates roughly 6B people have barely touched LLMs; 1–2B are casual chatbot users; about 20M professionals in math & code are watching months-long projects complete with a single prompt; and only ~5,000 people inside frontier labs are seeing agent swarms mint zero-days, run cyber operations at machine speed, and advance frontier mathematics in ways human reviewers can barely keep up with. His conclusion: the gap isn't just wide, it's accelerating. "I was writing code manually by hand, typing memorized computer code commands into a code editor character by character, occasionally pressing Tab to autocomplete a little chunk of code" — that was less than a year ago. The funnel is driven by ambition of the problem, the jaggedness of LLM capability (peaks where tasks are digital, verifiable, and economically valuable), and raw access tier.
https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046

Karpathy 发布了本期传播最广的推文之一：对 AI 能力漏斗的详细拆解。他估计约 60 亿人几乎未接触过 LLM；10–20 亿人是偶尔使用免费 ChatGPT 类产品的普通用户；约 2000 万从事数学和代码工作的专业人员正亲眼目睹几个月的项目被一个 prompt 完成；而只有约 5000 名顶级实验室内部人员才能看到 agent 集群以机器速度发动网络攻击、推进前沿数学研究，速度快到人类审查员已难以跟上。他的结论是：这个差距不仅巨大，而且还在加速扩大。"不到一年前，我还在手动逐字输入代码，偶尔按 Tab 自动补全一小段。" 这一漏斗由问题的雄心规模、LLM 能力的"锯齿状"特征（在数字化、可验证且具有经济价值的任务上达到峰值），以及访问层级共同驱动。

**No Priors — Frontier Chips for Frontier AI Labs, with Walter Goodwin, Founder/CEO of Fractile**

**The Takeaway:** The chip market's next frontier isn't more FLOPs — it's unlocking orders-of-magnitude more memory bandwidth, which is the real bottleneck preventing AI agents from running at the speeds that would make them genuinely transformative.

**摘要：** 芯片市场的下一个前沿不是更多的算力，而是释放数量级更高的内存带宽——这才是阻碍 AI agent 以真正变革性速度运行的真正瓶颈。

Walter Goodwin, founder and CEO of Fractile, has been building fast inference chips since 2022 — before "inference" was even the mainstream framing. His core argument: NVIDIA, AMD, Google TPUs, Meta MTIA, and even OpenAI's Jalapeno all share the same fundamental architectural bets (HBM memory, Tensor Cores, TSMC packaging). Fractile's differentiation is going end-to-end — front-end architecture, physical design, advanced packaging — under one roof, with about 150 people. That full-stack ownership lets them close the loop between workload observation and chip design in ways that handoff-dependent models can't match.

Fractile 创始人兼 CEO Walter Goodwin 自 2022 年便开始构建快速推理芯片——彼时"推理"甚至还不是主流叙事框架。他的核心论点是：NVIDIA、AMD、Google TPU、Meta MTIA，乃至 OpenAI 的 Jalapeno，在架构上有着相同的基本押注（HBM 内存、Tensor Core、台积电封装）。Fractile 的差异化在于从前端架构、物理设计到先进封装全部自研，约 150 人覆盖整个技术栈。这种全栈自有能力让他们能够以依赖外包的模式所无法企及的速度，在观察工作负载变化和芯片设计之间形成闭环。

His most contrarian point: the biggest limitation of current fast inference chips (like Groq) isn't speed — they are blindingly fast — it's that they rely on SRAM, which is high-bandwidth but tiny in capacity. Long-context inference and large agent sessions still flip back to GPU. Fractile's bet for their next platform (ramping second half of next year) is combining the bandwidth advantages of SRAM-based chips with the capacity of DRAM, via proprietary memory integration work developed quietly over the last two years.

他最具反向思考的观点：当前快速推理芯片（如 Groq）的最大限制不是速度——它们的速度已经极快——而是依赖 SRAM，后者带宽高但容量极小。长上下文推理和大型 agent 会话仍需切回 GPU 处理。Fractile 下一代平台（明年下半年量产）的押注是：通过过去两年悄然研发的专有内存集成技术，将基于 SRAM 芯片的带宽优势与 DRAM 的容量优势相结合。

On AI diffusion into chip design itself, Goodwin is cautiously optimistic but concrete: "It's equivalent of the frontier model for the chip space is if you can just find a way to structurally carve out a three to six months advantage, you will be winning all of those deployments." He believes design cycles can compress significantly with AI assistance, but physical reality — fab cycle times of 3–5 months, 3–5 year amortization windows for deployed chips — means the competitive edge comes from betting smarter and faster, not from shipping new silicon every quarter.

在 AI 渗透芯片设计本身这一问题上，Goodwin 谨慎乐观但态度具体："在芯片领域，这相当于 frontier 模型的逻辑——如果你能找到一种方式，在结构上持续保持 3 到 6 个月的领先，你就能赢得所有这些部署。" 他认为 AI 辅助可以显著压缩设计周期，但物理现实——晶圆厂周期 3–5 个月、已部署芯片的 3–5 年摊销窗口——意味着竞争优势来自更聪明更快速的押注，而非每季度推出新硅片。
