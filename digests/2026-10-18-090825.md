**Box CEO Aaron Levie** made a compelling case that AI is *expanding* the importance of software engineering, not shrinking it. In response to "end of software engineering" predictions, Levie argues the opposite is happening: because agents can use tools, work with computers, write software on the fly, and process data at scale, we can now apply "software" to problems that were never previously digitizable — from novel trading strategies in banking to research workflows in life sciences to supply chain automation in manufacturing. His conclusion: "Yes we're not going to be coding manually in the future. But almost everything will be code, which means those that understand it are in a fantastic position."

Box CEO Aaron Levie 对外界预言"软件工程终结"的论断进行了有力反驳。他认为，恰恰相反，AI 正在扩大软件工程的重要性——由于 agent 可以使用工具、操作计算机、即时编写代码并高速处理数据，我们现在可以将"软件"应用于此前永远无法数字化的问题，从银行的新型交易策略，到生命科学的研究流程，再到制造业的供应链自动化。他的结论是："我们未来确实不会再手动编写代码了，但几乎所有事情都将是代码——这意味着懂得这些的人处于极其有利的位置。"

## PODCASTS

**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck** — "What Happens When Billions of AI Agents Hit Your Database? (Andy Pavlo)"

**The Takeaway:** Vector databases are now table stakes. The real frontier has shifted to a far messier challenge — what happens when AI agents autonomously create, modify, and occasionally delete your production databases at scale?

Andy Pavlo is as close to a "database god" as the industry has — Carnegie Mellon professor, whose courses trained a generation of engineers (and, as it turns out, the AI models themselves). He recently joined ClickHouse to found ClickHouse Labs. In this conversation with Matt Turck, Pavlo maps the evolution of databases in the AI age.

Two years ago, vector databases were the story — specialized systems enabling semantic search for the RAG/chatbot era. Today, Pavlo argues, vector search is just a table stake: "Pretty much every single database vendor had their own vector index within a year." The moat is gone.

What's replaced it is something stranger and more consequential. Agents are now full database citizens: they create databases, branch them, run tests on snapshots, and sometimes delete them. The Databricks/Neon acquisition surfaced a remarkable stat: the percentage of Neon databases created by agents had jumped from 30% to 80%. Database branching — copy-on-write snapshots that let agents safely test on a real copy of production data without touching the live system — is emerging as a key enabling primitive for safe agent autonomy at the infrastructure layer.

But the wild west is real. Pavlo: "Every month there's a story where someone lost their production database because the agent deleted something." He describes the current pace of AI development as "taking a cheetah, giving them much cocaine, and putting it in a Ferrari" — extraordinary speed, wielded by people who often don't know their database history.

The bottom line: the age of agents is forcing a complete rethink of database architecture — not just how data is queried, but how it is created, protected, and cleaned up when autonomous systems are done with it.

**结语 | 收官**

The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck 最新一期——"当数十亿 AI Agent 涌向你的数据库，会发生什么？"，嘉宾为 Andy Pavlo。

**核心洞察：** Vector database 时代已经结束，它已成为基础设施的标配。真正的前沿问题是：当 AI agent 大规模自主创建、修改、乃至删除生产数据库时，整个数据库架构该如何应对？

Andy Pavlo 是数据库领域最接近"神级人物"的存在——卡内基梅隆大学教授，其课程培养了整整一代工程师（以及 AI 模型本身）。他最近加入 ClickHouse 创建了 ClickHouse Labs。在这期与 Matt Turck 的对话中，Pavlo 梳理了 AI 时代数据库的演变轨迹。

两年前，vector database 是焦点——专为 RAG/聊天机器人时代的语义搜索而生。如今，Pavlo 认为 vector search 已是"标配"："几乎所有数据库厂商都在一年内推出了自己的 vector 索引。"护城河已消失。

取而代之的是更陌生、影响更深远的变化。Agent 现已成为数据库的完整参与者：创建数据库、做分支（branching）、在快照上运行测试，有时甚至删除数据库。Databricks/Neon 收购案披露了一个惊人数据：由 agent 创建的 Neon 数据库比例从 30% 跃升至 80%。数据库 branching——写时复制快照，让 agent 能在真实生产数据副本上安全测试而不影响线上系统——正在成为 agent 基础设施层安全自主运行的关键原语。

但"西部荒野"状态是真实的。Pavlo 说："每个月都有人因为 agent 删了什么东西而丢失生产数据库的故事。"他将当前 AI 开发的速度比作"给猎豹喂了大量可卡因再把它放进法拉利"——速度极为惊人，而操控者往往对数据库历史一无所知。

结论：AI agent 时代正在迫使数据库架构进行全面重构——不只是数据如何查询，而是数据如何被创建、如何被保护，以及当自主系统完成任务后如何被清理。
