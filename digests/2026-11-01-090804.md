### Box CEO Aaron Levie

Box CEO Aaron Levie laid out what he calls the clearest litmus test for AI adoption: if you could have done what you're doing before AI existed, you're probably not pushing it hard enough. Six months ago, the best AI offered was incremental speed. But we've entered a new phase where AI should produce qualitatively different outcomes — fully functional prototypes with meaningfully better features, architectures that pursue performance gains you never would have attempted, research that covers every available fact across every analytical dimension, and business processes that learn from all past failures in ways no human team could. Levie argues that AI is becoming smarter than us on most dimensions, and when combined with what humans bring, the results shouldn't just be faster — they should be fundamentally different.

### Latent Space: Using Agents for Long-Horizon Financial Work — Aman Singh, Arca

**The Takeaway:** At AI-native wealth management firm Arca, agents prompt people more than people prompt agents — and that inversion is the entire product.

Aman Singh leads the AI platform team at Arca, which recently raised $64 million to build an AI-native wealth management firm. Arca isn't selling AI tools to advisors — it *is* the advisory firm itself, which means it can standardize how financial work happens in ways no SaaS company can. Clients never interact with AI directly; they simply experience a level of service that wasn't previously possible, delivered through human advisors freed from back-office work. The firm uses tiered models — Opus-class for complex tax analysis, lighter models for background tasks — with all routing handled invisibly.

What makes Arca's architecture compelling is its approach to long-horizon work. Financial processes can span 10 days with multiple actors. Rather than one persistent agent, fresh agents wake up to handle new signals and ramp up quickly using a persistent memory system tracking current state, pending actions, ownership, and long-term goals. A nightly meta-agent reflects on real-world traces and surfaces suggested improvements to the harness — though a human engineer still reviews prompt changes before shipping, because "agents will make really nasty prompt changes if you leave them to it."

Safety is built at capability edges: human-in-the-loop for all money movement, LLM-based verification on every database write, prompt injection detection at data ingestion boundaries, and consensus model checks before anything leaves the system. Compliance comes through auditability by default — every action, by agents or humans alike, produces a timestamped change log.

> "We're actually not a SaaS company. We are the firm ourselves. Agents prompt people more than people prompt agents — and it is a proactive kind of service that is running for them twenty-four/seven."

**Latent Space 播客：长周期金融工作中的 Agent 实践 — Aman Singh，Arca**

**核心洞察：** 在 AI 原生财富管理公司 Arca，是 agent 在驱动人类行动，而不是人类在驱动 agent——这个反转，就是整个产品的本质。

Aman Singh 领导 Arca 的 AI 平台团队。Arca 近期完成了 6400 万美元融资，打造 AI 原生财富管理公司。Arca 不是向顾问销售 AI 工具的 SaaS 公司——它本身就是那家顾问公司，这意味着它能以 SaaS 公司做不到的方式标准化工作流程。客户从不直接与 AI 交互，他们只是体验到一种前所未有的服务水准，由从繁琐后台工作中解放出来的人类顾问传递。系统使用分层模型——复杂税务分析用 Opus 级别，后台任务用轻量模型——所有路由对用户完全透明。

Arca 架构最吸引人的地方在于它对长周期工作的处理方式。金融流程可能跨越 10 天，涉及多个参与者。不是一个持久运行的 agent，而是不断有新 agent 被唤起处理新信号，并通过持久记忆系统快速掌握客户现状、待办事项、责任归属和长期目标。每晚，一个元 agent 会分析真实 agent 的执行轨迹，提出改进建议——但在真正上线前，仍由人类工程师审核 prompt 变更，因为"如果你放手让 agent 改 prompt，它们会改得一团糟。"

安全机制建立在能力边界处：所有资金操作都需人工确认，每次数据库写入都有 LLM 验证，数据进入时检测 prompt 注入，数据输出前用共识模型审查。合规性通过默认可审计性实现——agent 或人类的每一个操作都会生成带时间戳的变更日志。

> "我们其实不是一家 SaaS 公司。我们就是那家公司本身。是 agent 在推动人去行动，而不是人在推动 agent——这是一种为客户全天候运转的主动服务。"
