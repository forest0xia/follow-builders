**No Priors — Tokenization, Agentic Trading, Harmonic: Robinhood's Mission to Expand Ownership with CEO Vlad Tenev**

**The Takeaway:** Robinhood is transforming from a commission-free trading app into a global ownership infrastructure company — one that wants to put private company equity, tokenized real-world assets, and AI-powered trading into the hands of ordinary people worldwide.

Vlad Tenev grew up in Bulgaria during the 2,000% inflation crisis of 1997, watching his grandfather convert his salary immediately into copper cookware to store value. That formative experience with a broken monetary system gave him a visceral understanding of why broad ownership matters — and became the philosophical foundation for Robinhood.

Today, Robinhood operates in 120+ countries and has launched Robinhood Chain, its own blockchain for real-world assets that has become one of the fastest-growing chains by developer activity. Stock tokens — tokenized US equities — can now flow globally through DeFi protocols, with the vision of enabling anyone with a smartphone in Africa or South America to access US markets at incredibly low cost.

On AI, Tenev is building on two fronts. At Robinhood, the company launched an MCP-based Agentic Trading platform that has attracted 150,000+ accounts and 30 million tool calls per day — letting users build automated strategies with the sophistication of quant firms. At Harmonic, his co-founded startup, the team built Aristotle, a math AI that won gold at the International Math Olympiad, and is now being used to tackle unsolved problems like Erdős conjectures. Tenev's concern: the current wave of AI math progress is too centralized, run by big labs with secret models in proprietary data centers. His preferred future is one where individuals discover math with these tools — the same philosophy as democratizing finance.

> "If you wanted to invest in a large language model a couple years ago and you wanted exposure to OpenAI or Anthropic, you're basically out of luck as an individual investor."

That gap is what Robinhood Ventures aims to close, with publicly traded venture funds (OpenAI, Crusoe, and others already in Fund I) and a YC partnership for Fund II to give retail investors access at the seed and Series A stage.
