**Latent Space — Inside the AI Training Data Market — Gabe Greenberg, G2i**

**The Takeaway:** The human data market for AI training has quietly grown from gig work into a high-skill, partnership-driven industry — and the companies that figured this out earliest are now processing hundreds of millions of dollars in revenue with no VC backing.

Gabe Greenberg is the CEO of G2I, a company that builds training data infrastructure for frontier AI labs. G2I scaled from $15M to roughly $100M in revenue in a single year — profitably, without venture capital. Their customers are the five or six frontier labs building the most powerful models in the world.

The business has changed dramatically. Early RLHF-era human labeling relied on large pools of mid-level workers choosing which of two samples read better. Now G2I works with staff-level engineers building full reinforcement learning environments — complex, multi-layered datasets that include verifiers, tasks, and seed data. The hardest problem: tasks need to be just hard enough that they're a few steps beyond what the model can do today. Too hard and they become evals. Too easy and they're worthless.

What the labs actually want today has also shifted. "A year and a half ago, they wanted volume and correctness," Greenberg explains. "Now there's so much more about behavior and taste." He points to Claude's Opus as an example of the difference taste makes in a finished model.

One counterintuitive finding: most labs don't build in-house data operations despite the obvious incentives, because managing thousands of contractors creates legal, security, and operational overhead that researchers want nothing to do with. "Think about how many lawsuits you would get if they knew the end customer was X, Y, and Z." The complexity is a moat for vendors like G2I.

Looking ahead, Greenberg sees robotics and 3D world models as the next wave, along with mechanical engineering and chip design — domains where "you can still sign a six-month contract today."

**核心要点：** AI 训练的人工数据市场已悄然从零工经济演变成一个高技能、以合作伙伴关系为驱动的行业——最早想清楚这件事的公司，如今已在无需 VC 背书的情况下实现数亿美元的年收入。

Gabe Greenberg 是 G2I 的 CEO，该公司为前沿 AI 实验室构建训练数据基础设施。G2I 在一年内从 1500 万美元增长到约 1 亿美元收入，且保持盈利，没有风险投资支持。他们的客户是世界上正在构建最强大模型的五六家前沿实验室。

业务已发生巨大变化。早期 RLHF 时代的人工标注依赖大量中级工人来判断哪个样本更好。如今 G2I 与资深工程师合作，构建完整的强化学习环境——包含 verifier、任务和种子数据的复杂多层数据集。最难的问题在于：任务需要恰好比模型今天能做到的事情难几步，让它可训练。太难就变成了 eval，太容易则毫无价值。

实验室的需求也在转变。"一年半前，他们要的是数量和正确性，"Greenberg 说，"现在，行为和品味变得更加重要。"他以 Claude 的 Opus 为例，说明品味对最终模型质量的影响。

一个反直觉的发现：尽管建立内部数据团队有明显动机，但大多数实验室并没有这样做——因为管理数千名承包商带来的法律、安全和运营负担是研究人员不想碰的。"想想如果他们知道最终客户是 X、Y、Z，你会面临多少诉讼。"这种复杂性反而成为 G2I 等供应商的护城河。

展望未来，Greenberg 认为机器人、3D 世界模型以及机械工程和芯片设计将是下一波浪潮——这些领域"今天还能签六个月的合同"。
