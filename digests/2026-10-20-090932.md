**AI & I by Every — "Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent"**

**The Takeaway:** Personal agents are compelling for tinkerers, but they die when you stop maintaining them — and the real unlock comes when a whole company pools its energy into one shared agent that gets smarter for everyone.

Every CEO Dan Shipper and head of platform Willie Williams trace their company's arc from chaos to clarity on AI agents. Starting in early 2026, Every gave everyone their own personal "Open Claw" — a Claude-based agent living in Slack. For a while, it was magical: colleagues discovered how others prompted, what workflows they'd built, how AI could sneak into every corner of work. But cracks formed fast. Maintenance burden piled up. Agents broke when Mac minis lost power. People drifted to tools like Codex that just worked, and the personal agents quietly died.

The pivot to a single company agent — "Every Agent" — changed everything. Instead of each person investing in their own bot, collective energy compounds. The agent learns Every's workflows, voice, and context over time; a new hire doesn't need to onboard it from scratch. Willie makes a pointed observation: "remembering which agent had which expertise was annoying and hard." One agent solves that.

Willie also shares how his own engineering management has changed in the AI era: the job is still fundamentally about people and removing bottlenecks, but his leverage has multiplied. He runs a custom feed ("Tend") that consolidates Slack, Notion, meetings, and customer reports — giving him field marshal visibility over a large team without drowning in meetings. "The real magic now is... you can put an agent, like, look at this every five minutes and see if there's an anomaly. If there's an anomaly, do intelligent routing."

On the future: both expect a clean split between professional and personal agents. At work, one company agent per org will win — it accumulates context that a personal agent can never catch up to. At home, truly personal agents will thrive precisely *because* they're yours.

**核心洞察：** 个人 agent 对喜欢折腾的人很有吸引力，但一旦停止维护就会死掉 —— 真正的突破是整个团队把精力汇聚到一个共享 agent 上，让它对所有人越来越聪明。

Every CEO Dan Shipper 与平台负责人 Willie Williams 回顾了公司在 AI agent 上从混沌走向清晰的历程。2026 年初，Every 为每个人都配备了自己的「Open Claw」—— 一个基于 Claude 的 Slack agent。起初效果惊艳：同事们互相发现彼此的提示词技巧、工作流设计，AI 渗透进工作的每个角落。但裂缝很快出现 —— 维护负担积累，Mac mini 断电就会导致 agent 消失，人们逐渐迁移到 Codex 等「开箱即用」的工具，个人 agent 悄然死去。

转向单一公司 agent「Every Agent」后，局面彻底改变。集体的精力不再分散，而是不断复利。agent 随时间学习 Every 的工作流、语气和背景；新员工入职也无需从零训练它。Willie 一针见血：「记住哪个 agent 擅长什么这件事既烦人又费力。」一个 agent 解决了这个问题。

Willie 还分享了 AI 时代工程管理的变化：工作本质仍然是关于人和消除瓶颈，但他的杠杆大幅放大。他运行一个自定义信息流「Tend」，整合了 Slack、Notion、会议和客户报告 —— 让他以「战场指挥官」的视野俯瞰整个团队，而无需溺于会议。「真正的魔法在于……你可以放一个 agent，每五分钟检查一次有没有异常，发现异常就做智能路由。」

对于未来：两人都预期个人与职业 agent 将出现清晰的分化。职场上，每个公司一个 agent 将胜出 —— 它积累的上下文是个人 agent 永远追不上的。而在生活中，真正个人化的 agent 恰恰会因为「它是你的」而兴旺。
