### Madhu Guru — Sr. Director of AI at Meta

Madhu Guru, who previously led Gemini, Veo, and Nano at Google and now runs AI at Meta, shared a sharp diagnosis of why most enterprise AI programs fail. Three patterns: relying on old-school playbooks (the CEO appoints a trusted lieutenant, who pulls a team together — but the skills required for AI are fundamentally different from incremental product development), under-investing in evals (most enterprises still don't understand what "good" looks like here), and building AI *for* the company rather than *with* it (centralized platform teams produce tools disconnected from real workflows, leading to grudging adoption and no meaningful productivity gains).

His prescription: hire leaders who have actually shipped AI products, make evals a first-class citizen, and embed your best AI builders inside the functions they're trying to transform.
https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098448235048378456

曾在 Google 主导 Gemini、Veo 和 Nano、现任 Meta AI 高级总监的 Madhu Guru，犀利分析了大多数企业 AI 项目为何失败。三大症结：沿用旧有打法（CEO 指定心腹副手，拼凑团队——但 AI 所需的能力与渐进式产品开发截然不同）；在 evals 上投入不足（大多数企业根本不明白"好的 evals"是什么样子）；以及以"为公司做 AI"替代"与公司共建 AI"（集中式平台团队开发的工具脱离了真实工作流，只换来被迫采用，毫无实质生产力提升）。

他的解方：聘用真正交付过 AI 产品的领导者，让 evals 成为一等公民，把最优秀的 AI builder 嵌入他们要改造的业务部门内部。
https://x.com/realmadhuguru/status/2098448235048378456

### No Priors — "Coinbase's Everything Exchange: Agentic Finance, Stablecoins, and Tokenization with CEO Brian Armstrong"

**The Takeaway:** Crypto rails are becoming the essential infrastructure for AI agents — and Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong believes there will be more agents in the economy than humans, with an agentic economy that eventually surpasses the human one.

**Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 的核心观点：** Crypto 基础设施正在成为 AI agent 不可或缺的基础——Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 相信，经济体系中的 agent 数量终将超过人类，agentic economy 的规模也将最终超越人类经济。

Armstrong sees Coinbase operating on three major fronts. First, the "Everything Exchange" — a single platform where traders can access stocks, commodities, crypto, derivatives, and prediction markets across more jurisdictions as regulatory clarity emerges. Second, stablecoins: payments that move "at the speed of information, under one second and under one cent to move money anywhere in the world," growing even when Bitcoin prices fall. Third, what he calls "Agentic Finance" or AIFi: AI agents that need their own financial accounts, not tied to a human's KYC process.

Armstrong 将 Coinbase 的布局锁定在三个核心方向。第一，"Everything Exchange"——一个让交易者在同一平台访问股票、大宗商品、加密货币、衍生品和预测市场的交易场所，随着监管日趋明朗，覆盖的市场也在持续扩大。第二，稳定币支付：以"信息速度移动的资金，跨境转账低于一秒、不足一美分"，即便在比特币价格下跌时也持续增长。第三，他所说的"Agentic Finance"或 AIFi：AI agent 需要拥有自己的金融账户，而不受制于人类的 KYC 流程。

On why crypto is specifically suited for AI: 76% of agent ecommerce transactions Coinbase is seeing are under 30 cents — below the minimum floor for traditional card networks. "We don't want the AIs to be unbanked," Armstrong said. "If we wanna bank the AIs, they deserve financial services as well."

关于为何加密货币特别适合 AI：Coinbase 观测到 76% 的 agent 电商交易金额低于 30 美分——低于传统卡支付的最低手续费门槛。"我们不希望 AI 成为没有银行账户的人，"Armstrong 说，"如果我们想让 AI 接入金融体系，它们同样值得拥有金融服务。"

Inside Coinbase, Armstrong is building toward recursive self-improvement: every time an agent touches a service repository, it ingests a "brain" — a living document of all incidents, financial controls, A/B tests, and pull request history for that service. When a human reviews and corrects the agent's work, that correction feeds back into the brain, improving future outputs. "The accept rate for one-shotted PRs just starts to tick up over time, and you've now got a recursive self-improvement system," he said.

在 Coinbase 内部，Armstrong 正在构建递归式自我改进机制：每当 agent 触碰某个服务代码库时，它会读取一份"大脑"——记录该服务所有事故、财务控制、A/B 测试和 PR 历史的动态文档。当人类审阅并纠正 agent 的输出时，这些修正会反馈进"大脑"，持续提升未来的输出质量。"一次性命中的 PR 接受率会随时间不断提升，于是你就拥有了一套递归自我改进系统，"他说。

Armstrong also outlined New Limit, his longevity biotech venture, which has demonstrated successful epigenetic reprogramming of human cells in humanized mouse models. Phase one clinical trials in alcohol-related liver disease are planned for next year — a beachhead for a longer-term vision of reprogramming cells across multiple tissue types to restore the function they had when younger.

Armstrong 还介绍了他的长寿生物科技项目 New Limit，该公司已在人源化小鼠模型中成功验证了人类细胞的表观遗传重编程。针对酒精性肝病的一期临床试验计划于明年启动——这是一个更长远愿景的滩头阵地：对多种组织类型的细胞进行重编程，使其恢复年轻时的功能。
