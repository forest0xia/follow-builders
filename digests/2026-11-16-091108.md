**Guillermo Rauch** (CEO @Vercel)

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch announced `xeet`, a hermetic, pixel-perfect X post renderer written in safe Rust and compiled to Wasm. It renders posts to PNG in roughly 1ms — 100-1000x faster than spinning up a browser — while reproducing Blink text shaping, RTL bidi, 28 Chirp font subsets, and CJK/emoji fallbacks with full fidelity. What's architecturally notable: xeet is structured as an "autorepo," where an oracle verifies tens of thousands of text layout rules against Chrome, and a CI canary watches the original X implementation, filing signed PRs for agents to act on. In a lighter post, Rauch also noted that AI models have cracked one of computer science's classically hard problems — naming things. A CLI tool he built to diagnose system slowdowns was named `hog` by the model, which he loved.

**No Priors — Tokenization, Agentic Trading, Harmonic: Robinhood's Mission to Expand Ownership with CEO Vlad Tenev**

The Takeaway: Vlad Tenev believes broadly distributed ownership — from birth to retirement — is the antidote to social instability in an AI-disrupted economy, and Robinhood is building the infrastructure to make it real.

Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev traces his conviction about democratized ownership back to childhood in Bulgaria, where his grandfather stored savings in copper cookware against 2000% annualized inflation. That visceral experience of broken financial systems became a north star: a society without broad ownership is fragile, and giving more people a stake in the economy is both a moral and market imperative.

Today Robinhood is pursuing that mission at multiple layers. Robinhood Chain, its own blockchain built for real-world assets, is reportedly the fastest-growing chain by developer activity. Stock tokens — tokenized US equities with full DeFi composability — launched earlier this year, with developers already building stock-token-linked NFTs, meme coin pairings, and options-like smart contracts. The goal: let anyone with a smartphone in any of 120+ countries access US markets the same way stablecoins democratized dollar access.

On agentic finance, Tenev says the data is unambiguous: over 150,000 customers have opened agentic trading accounts since Robinhood's MCP launched, generating 30 million tool calls per day. The new Robinhood Agents product embeds the full agentic harness inside a Robinhood account with loops, portfolio rebalancing, and an app store for third-party data like satellite imagery of parking lots. The analogy Tenev reaches for: "In the same way that it's made it easier for anyone to be a software engineer, Robinhood Agents can make it easier for more people to be quant traders."

On Harmonic, his AI math startup, Tenev pushes back on centralized AI labs hoarding scientific discovery: "It's too centralized — AI companies with their big data centers and their employees using secret models to do all this work." He believes the same breakthroughs will be more accepted and more likely to happen when distributed to individuals. Last year, Harmonic's Aristotle model earned a gold medal at the International Math Olympiad.

Finally, on Trump accounts: Robinhood was selected as the sole initial trustee to seed every child born from January 2025 with $1,000 in a diversified ETF. Tenev's stated goal is to lift household stock ownership from 65% to 95%.
