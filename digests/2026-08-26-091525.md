### Training Data: "Parallel's Parag Agrawal: Building a New Web for AI Agents"

**The Takeaway:** Former Twitter CEO Parag Agrawal is building Parallel Web Systems to reinvent search for AI agents — and his most contrarian bet is that human click data is a bug, not a feature.

Parag Agrawal left running one of the world's largest social networks and returned as a founder with a specific thesis: agents will perform searches at 1000x the rate humans do, which means the entire infrastructure and business model of the web needs to be rebuilt for that new customer. Parallel is his vehicle for that.

His first non-obvious insight: start with a search *agent* before building a search *engine*. By letting the agent crawl pages in real time during deep research sessions (where users tolerate minute-long waits), Parallel could build its index incrementally while immediately serving real customer needs — competing against "humans sitting on search engines" rather than Google on day one.

On the economic model of the internet: Agrawal is sanguine that ads won't survive the agent era. Agents don't click ads, can't be shown display banners, and don't produce the "lazy human" behavior (two-word typo queries, preference for fast-loading pages over authoritative PDFs) that the entire SEO ecosystem was built around. His proposed replacement: Shapley value-based attribution for content owners — a game-theoretic framework that estimates how much each piece of content contributed to an agent's final output, then compensates the content owner proportionally. "If you have unique differentiated data, you get paid more. If a banker in an expensive job reads your data versus my retired dad reads your data, the banker ends up paying more."

His most concrete signal: Parallel just announced a partnership with Google Cloud as a search and grounding provider for Gemini models — meaning when enterprises build agents on GCP and attach web search, Parallel is now a first-class option alongside Google Search.

On the scale of the transition: "I recently saw Cloudflare say that in their monitoring of web traffic, AI traffic is about the same as human traffic in terms of page reads." We're still early, but the crossover is happening.

**核心结论：** 前 Twitter CEO Parag Agrawal 正在通过 Parallel Web Systems 重构 AI agent 时代的搜索基础设施——他最具颠覆性的判断是：人类点击数据是一个 bug，而不是特性。

Agrawal 卸任全球最大社交网络之一的掌舵人后，以创业者身份回归，带着一个明确的论题：agent 执行搜索的频率将是人类的1000倍，这意味着整个网络的基础设施和商业模式都需要为这个新客户重建。Parallel 就是他实现这一愿景的载体。

他的第一个反直觉洞察：先做搜索 *agent*，再建搜索 *引擎*。通过让 agent 在深度研究会话中实时抓取页面（用户愿意等待几分钟），Parallel 能一边服务真实客户需求，一边逐步积累索引——从"与人类坐在搜索引擎前"竞争开始，而不是从第一天就与 Google 正面硬刚。

关于互联网的经济模型：Agrawal 认为广告无法在 agent 时代存活。Agent 不点广告、不看横幅、也不会产生那种"懒人人类"行为（两个词的错别字查询、偏好加载快的页面而非权威 PDF）——而 SEO 生态系统的整个基础恰恰建立在这些行为之上。他提出的替代方案：基于 Shapley 值的内容归因——一种博弈论框架，估算每段内容对 agent 最终输出的贡献，然后按比例补偿内容所有者。"如果你的数据具有独特性，你就能获得更多报酬。如果是一位高薪银行家读了你的数据，而不是我退休的父亲，那么银行家那次读取就会支付更高的费用。"

他最具说服力的信号：Parallel 刚刚宣布与 Google Cloud 达成合作，成为 Gemini 模型的搜索与 grounding 提供商——这意味着企业在 GCP 上构建 agent 并附加网络搜索时，Parallel 现已成为与 Google 搜索并列的一线选项。
