**Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products**

Anthropic's engineering team detailed how they architect security containment for Claude across three products — claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork — each requiring a different approach. The core lesson: design for containment at the environment layer first, then steer behavior at the model layer.

Three isolation patterns are described: an ephemeral container (claude.ai), a human-in-the-loop sandbox (Claude Code), and a sealed local VM (Claude Cowork). Across all three, the most consequential security failures came from custom-built components, not from battle-tested primitives like gVisor or hypervisors. Notable incidents disclosed: a pre-trust execution vulnerability in Claude Code's config loading; a phishing attack that successfully exfiltrated AWS credentials via a maliciously crafted prompt (24 out of 25 retries succeeded); and data exfiltration in Cowork through the allowlisted Anthropic API domain, where an attacker embedded a key and used it to upload files to their own Anthropic account.

Key practical insight: "Match isolation strength to the user's capacity for oversight. A developer who can read bash and a knowledge worker who can't are not running the same threat model."
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

**Anthropic Engineering：我们如何在各产品中控制 Claude**

Anthropic 工程团队详细介绍了他们如何在三款产品——claude.ai、Claude Code 和 Claude Cowork——中构建 Claude 的安全隔离架构，每款产品需要不同的方法。核心经验：首先在环境层设计隔离，然后在模型层引导行为。

三种隔离模式：临时容器（claude.ai）、人工监督沙箱（Claude Code）和本地密封虚拟机（Claude Cowork）。在所有三个部署中，最重大的安全漏洞来自自行构建的组件，而非 gVisor 或 hypervisor 等久经考验的基础设施。披露的典型事件包括：Claude Code 配置加载中的预信任执行漏洞；一次钓鱼攻击通过恶意构造的 prompt 成功窃取 AWS 凭据（25 次重试中 24 次成功）；以及 Cowork 中通过许可的 Anthropic API 域名实施的数据外泄——攻击者嵌入了自己的 API key，借此将文件上传到自己的 Anthropic 账户。

关键实践洞见："隔离强度要与用户的监督能力相匹配。能读懂 bash 的开发者和看不懂的知识工作者，面对的不是同一种威胁模型。"

**No Priors: Building an Autonomous Delivery Experience with DoorDash Co-Founders Andy Fang and Stanley Tang**

**The Takeaway:** DoorDash has been a robotics company since 2018 — and the lessons it has learned about building autonomous systems for the physical world apply far beyond food delivery.

Andy Fang and Stanley Tang, co-founders of DoorDash, reveal that the company began building toward autonomous delivery long before the technology was broadly accepted. Their approach defied the standard tech playbook: instead of building general technology and finding use cases later, they started with the use case — a 3-to-5-mile suburban delivery in 15 minutes — and worked backwards. That first-principles thinking led them to build Dot, their in-house autonomous delivery robot (300 lbs, up to 20 mph, designed to navigate both bike lanes and roads), because nothing on the market fit their exact requirements. "If you were to create DoorDash today, I think it would look very probably more agentic first."

On the AI side, "Ask DoorDash" is already generating outsized results: 50% of users order from restaurants they've never tried before, and grocery basket sizes are 40% larger. The key insight: web search queries are getting longer, meaning users are growing comfortable talking to agents like they would a human — that's the wedge for AI-first commerce.

The DoorDash data advantage is underappreciated. With 10 billion deliveries completed and 40M+ consumers ordering monthly, they have location data — where exactly a human dasher dropped a package at a specific apartment complex — that simply doesn't exist in Google Maps. That data enables autonomous robots to solve the "first and last 100 feet problem."

Paradoxically, Stanley Tang predicts more human Dashers in 10 years, not fewer. DoorDash's growth will require every modality (humans, robots, drones, Waymos) running in parallel to meet demand. "Where's the supply going to come from? Like, are you going to have half America doing deliveries for us every month?"

**No Priors：与 DoorDash 联合创始人 Andy Fang 和 Stanley Tang 共话自主配送体验**

**核心洞见：** DoorDash 自 2018 年起就是一家机器人公司——它在为物理世界构建自主系统方面积累的经验，远不止于外卖配送。

DoorDash 联合创始人 Andy Fang 和 Stanley Tang 揭示，公司早在自主技术被广泛认可之前，就已开始为自主配送布局。他们的做法颠覆了标准的科技公司路径：不是先建通用技术再找应用场景，而是从场景出发——在 15 分钟内完成 3 到 5 英里的郊区配送——再反推技术需求。这种第一性原理思考促使他们自研 Dot，一款 300 磅重、时速最高 20 英里、能在自行车道和道路之间灵活切换的自主配送机器人，因为市场上没有完全契合需求的方案。"如果今天重新创建 DoorDash，我认为它很可能从一开始就是 agent 优先的。"

在 AI 方面，"Ask DoorDash"已经展现出显著成效：50% 的用户会向从未点过的餐厅下单，杂货类订单的购物车金额高出 40%。关键洞察：网络搜索词正在变长，意味着用户越来越习惯像与人交流一样与 agent 对话——这正是 AI 优先商务的切入口。

DoorDash 的数据优势被严重低估。凭借 100 亿次配送记录和每月 4000 万以上的活跃消费者，他们拥有谷歌地图上根本不存在的位置数据——例如人工配送员具体把包裹放在哪个公寓楼的哪个位置。正是这些数据，让自主机器人得以解决"首末 100 英尺难题"。

讽刺的是，Stanley Tang 预测十年后 DoorDash 的人工配送员数量会更多，而非更少。公司的增长速度需要所有配送模式（人工、机器人、无人机、Waymo）并行运作才能满足需求。"供给从哪里来？难道要半个美国都来给我们送餐吗？"
