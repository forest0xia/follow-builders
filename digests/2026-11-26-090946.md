**Training Data — "OpenAI's Noam Brown: Why Game Theory Didn't Lead to General Intelligence (Live at No Limit Finals)"**

**The Takeaway:** OpenAI researcher Noam Brown discovered that game theory — his entire career — wasn't the path to general intelligence, and had the intellectual honesty to pivot completely.

Noam Brown's journey from poker AI to OpenAI's reasoning team is a rare case of a researcher publicly abandoning his life's work in favor of what the evidence actually showed. After cracking poker with superhuman bots through counterfactual regret minimization (CFR), Brown moved to diplomacy — convinced it would be the hardest challenge for AI. It was there he discovered game theory's fatal flaw in real-world settings: it assumes everyone is rational. When his diplomacy bots started offering ultimatum-game-style lowball offers to opponents (technically optimal, humanly absurd), he realized the right path wasn't game theory at all — it was training models on vast amounts of human data to understand irrational, real-world behavior.

"My entire career up until that point had been about game theory... I had to wrestle with the fact that actually that was not the path to general intelligence."

The bigger insight he brought to OpenAI: the same logic that made game-playing AIs strong — making them *think before acting* — could unlock much more powerful reasoning in LLMs when combined with reinforcement learning on chain-of-thought. This became the core of o1. His test: for every new model, he asks it to build a poker bot. With recent models (he cites GPT-5.5 and beyond), they went from bad to 10–20x augmenting his own ability. But they still can't do it fully autonomously — they get lost in long-horizon planning and over-optimize irrelevant numerics.

His most provocative take: if you're a promising student right now, the best thing to do is drop out and join the labs. "Things are going fast — it's an exciting time. It's not a great time to sit on the sidelines."

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tRaFbDFBl_A

**要点：** OpenAI 研究员 Noam Brown 发现，博弈论——他整个职业生涯的核心——并不是通向通用智能的道路。面对证据，他有足够的智识诚实，彻底转向。

Noam Brown 从扑克 AI 到 OpenAI 推理团队的历程，是一位研究者罕见地公开放弃毕生工作、转而追随证据所指的案例。在通过反事实遗憾最小化（CFR）让 AI 超越人类扑克水平后，Brown 转战外交游戏，坚信这将是 AI 面临的最难挑战。正是在这里，他发现了博弈论在现实场景中的致命缺陷：它假设所有人都是理性的。当他的外交 AI 开始向对手提出最后通牒博弈式的极低报价（理论最优，但在人类看来荒谬可笑）时，他意识到正确的道路根本不是博弈论——而是在海量人类数据上训练模型，让它理解非理性的、真实世界的行为。

"我整个职业生涯都是关于博弈论的……我不得不面对这样一个事实：这根本不是通向通用智能的道路。"

他带到 OpenAI 的更大洞察：让博弈 AI 变强的同一逻辑——让它们在行动前先思考——当与链式思维上的强化学习结合时，可以在 LLM 中解锁强大得多的推理能力。这成为了 o1 的核心。他的测试指标：每当有新模型发布，他都会让它构建一个扑克机器人。在最近的模型中（他提到 GPT-5.5 及以后），它们从效果很差进步到将他的能力放大 10-20 倍。但它们仍然无法完全自主地完成任务——它们会在长期规划中迷失方向，并对无关的数值过度优化。

他最具挑战性的观点：如果你现在是一个有潜力的学生，最好的选择就是退学加入 AI 实验室。"时代发展很快——这是令人兴奋的时刻。现在不是站在场边观望的时候。"
