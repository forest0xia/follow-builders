**Claude Blog: Claude in Chrome is generally available**

Claude in Chrome is now generally available on every paid Claude plan, with a significant new capability: Claude can now take actions autonomously in the browser without requiring user approval for every step. A safety classifier validates each action before it's performed to ensure it matches the original request. Anthropic reports that with the latest safeguards — including probes that scan web content for prompt injections and an automatic approval safety classifier — no prompt injection attacks succeeded against Claude Sonnet 5 or Opus 5, and only 0.3% succeeded against Fable 5. The rollout reflects months of hardening against prompt injection attacks, where malicious instructions hidden in web content try to redirect an AI agent's behavior. Enterprise admins can manage it in Organization Settings, limit access to approved domains, and Claude in Chrome is available for install from the Chrome Web Store.

Claude now has a dedicated browser built into Claude Cowork on the desktop app. When a task requires a website, a browser opens in the side panel and Claude navigates pages, reads content, clicks, and types — all without touching the user's own browser or data. **No Priors — Redefining Chip Architecture with Arm CEO Rene Haas**

**The Takeaway:** AI is not a threat to Arm's CPU business — it's the engine supercharging it, because every AI workload still needs a CPU to orchestrate the tokens, and Arm is positioned to be in every device from data centers to robots.

Arm CEO Rene Haas doesn't shy away from a provocative thesis: GPUs get the headlines, but CPUs were never going away. "There's no computing problem that's ever been invented that doesn't utilize and can't utilize the microprocessor. It is the heart of everything. All roads lead through it, around it, past it." As AI inference scales out and tokens multiply, something still has to orchestrate where they go — and that's the CPU.

Haas is not just talking theory. Arm recently shipped its first physical chip product (the Arm AGI CPU, developed with Meta), moving from pure IP licensing to joining the messy realities of supply chains, memory allocation, and packaging constraints. He describes the transition bluntly: "I remember coming to ARM in 2013 and thinking, no inventory, no RMA, no scrap, what's not to like?" Now they're building that muscle fast, with executives poached from Broadcom, Qualcomm, and Nvidia.

On AI in chip design, Haas is a believer but sets realistic expectations. Some 80-90% of Arm's engineers use AI daily — especially for verification and debugging, where the time savings are enormous. But RTL generation and physical design remain immature because the models are trained on public data, while the best chip knowledge is proprietary. His take: "If we were to shut it off, it's like being in the 1990s, you've got internet and you're now saying, only internet between the hours of two and four. There'd be anarchy." He's optimistic that chip-to-GDS design cycles could compress from 24-36 months to potentially sub-year within a decade.

On export controls and the geopolitics of chips, Haas takes a nuanced position — as an American citizen who runs a UK company with 30% of staff in the US. He supports domestic semiconductor manufacturing for national security reasons and calls it "critically important," while arguing the AI race is ultimately an "infinite game" where there's no single winner, making blanket restrictions a blunt tool with complex second-order effects.

**No Priors — 重新定义芯片架构：与 Arm CEO Rene Haas 的深度对话**

**核心观点：** AI 不是对 Arm CPU 业务的威胁，而是推动其腾飞的引擎——因为每个 AI 工作负载仍然需要 CPU 来调度 token，而 Arm 已准备好进入从数据中心到机器人的每一个终端。

Arm CEO Rene Haas 毫不回避一个反直觉的论点：GPU 抢尽风头，但 CPU 从未离场。"从来没有一个计算问题不需要、不能利用微处理器。它是一切的核心。所有道路都经过它、绕过它、从它身旁穿过。"随着 AI 推理不断扩展、token 数量激增，仍然需要有东西来调度它们的去向——这就是 CPU 的工作。

Haas 并非纸上谈兵。Arm 最近推出了首款自研芯片产品（与 Meta 合作开发的 Arm AGI CPU），从纯 IP 授权转向亲身经历供应链、内存分配和封装约束的"混战"。他坦诚描述这一转变："我记得 2013 年来到 Arm 时心想，没有库存、没有退货、没有报废，有什么不好的？"如今他们正快速积累这方面的能力，从 Broadcom、Qualcomm 和 Nvidia 挖来了高管团队。

在芯片设计中应用 AI 方面，Haas 是坚定的信徒，但也保持现实。Arm 约 80-90% 的工程师每天使用 AI，尤其在验证和调试环节，节省的时间巨大。但 RTL 生成和物理设计仍不成熟，因为模型训练数据主要来自公开资料，而最优质的芯片设计知识是私有的。他说："如果我们把它关掉，就像回到 1990 年代，你有了互联网，然后被告知只能在下午两点到四点之间上网。会天下大乱的。"他乐观地认为，芯片到 GDS 的设计周期可能在十年内从 24-36 个月压缩到不到一年。

在出口管制和芯片地缘政治方面，Haas 表明了一个细致的立场——作为一名美国公民，他领导着一家英国公司，30% 的员工在美国。他支持出于国家安全考虑的国内半导体制造，并称其"至关重要"，同时认为 AI 竞赛归根结底是一场"无限游戏"，不存在单一赢家，这使得全面限制成为一个效果复杂的钝性工具。
