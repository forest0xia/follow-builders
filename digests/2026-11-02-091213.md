**Unsupervised Learning — Ep 95: Modal CEO on the Future of Inference, the Chaos of the GPU Market & Origins of the Sandbox Bet**

The Takeaway: The GPU compute market is still a Wild West of WhatsApp groups and hotel lobby handshakes — and the company that learned to thrive in that chaos is quietly crossing $1B in annual revenue.

Modal CEO Eric sat down with Redpoint GP Patrick Chase to explain how building infrastructure the hard way — custom file systems, custom container runtimes, no Kubernetes, no Docker — became Modal's deepest competitive moat. The GPU market, he says, remains shockingly unorganized: "I went to GTC last year... it was like an open air drug market, but like, GPUs. Walking around talking to people, trying to get GPUs, meeting people in hotel lobbies, exchanging numbers." Modal does two to three compute deals every week just to keep pace, aggregating capacity across 25 different clouds.

The Sandbox story is a masterclass in asymmetric bets. Modal launched code-execution sandboxes in 2023 as a low-cost experiment — the prototype took one engineer a few days. By 2025, when coding agents exploded, the product had to be rewritten five times just to handle scale. Sandboxes are now a $300M standalone run rate business, about a third of Modal's total revenue.

On software development with agents: roughly 95% of code merged at Modal is now agent-written. Counterintuitively, it is senior engineers — not juniors — who have gone "agent-maxing" the hardest. "For them, it's like giving them 10 interns. They've been constrained by typing speed for a long time, but they have all these ideas in their heads."

On the inference market: straight-down-the-middle token serving is commoditizing fast. The durable moat is a platform of adjacent primitives — sandboxes, reinforcement learning, training, batch processing — because "customers don't want single-trick ponies." Next bets: low-latency inference as a "CDN for inference," full VM support for Mac/Windows/Android/iOS, and deeper open-source collaboration on vLLM and SGLang.
https://www.youtube.com/@RedpointAI

**Unsupervised Learning — Ep 95：Modal CEO 谈 inference 的未来、GPU 市场的混沌与 Sandbox 赌注的起源**

核心洞察：GPU 算力市场至今仍是 WhatsApp 群和酒店大堂握手交易的蛮荒之地 —— 而在这片混沌中摸爬滚打并最终突破 10 亿美元年化营收的公司，就是 Modal。

Modal CEO Eric 与 Redpoint GP Patrick Chase 深度对话，讲述 Modal 如何通过"难啃的"基础设施建设 —— 自建文件系统、自建容器 runtime、不用 Kubernetes、不用 Docker —— 构建出极难复制的竞争护城河。他直言 GPU 市场的真实现状依然混乱不堪："我去年去参加了 GTC……感觉就像一个露天毒品市场，只不过卖的是 GPU。四处走、四处谈，在酒店大堂和人交换联系方式。"Modal 每周完成两到三笔算力采购协议，目前已整合超过 25 个云提供商的容量。

Sandbox 的故事是一堂关于不对称赌注的大师课。Modal 在 2023 年推出代码执行 Sandbox，当时只是一个低成本实验 —— 原型只需一名工程师花几天时间。到 2025 年 coding agent 爆发时，这个产品被重写了五次来应对规模化需求。如今，Sandbox 已独立实现 3 亿美元年化营收，约占 Modal 总营收的三分之一。

关于 agentic 软件开发：Modal 目前合并的代码中约有 95% 是由 agent 编写。出乎意料的是，走向"agent 极限使用"最彻底的是资深工程师，而非初级工程师。"对他们来说，这就像突然有了 10 个实习生。他们长期受限于打字速度，但脑子里其实有大量想法。"

关于 inference 市场：单纯的 token 服务正在快速商品化。真正持久的护城河在于一套相互关联的原语平台 —— Sandbox、强化学习、训练、批处理 —— 因为"客户不想要只会一招的工具"。下一步押注：以"inference CDN"为方向的低延迟 inference、支持 Mac/Windows/Android/iOS 的完整 VM 环境，以及在 vLLM 和 SGLang 上更深入的开源协作。
