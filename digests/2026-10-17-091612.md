**No Priors — "The Changing Physics of Cybersecurity and Drug Development with OpenAI President Greg Brockman"**

Greg Brockman, cofounder and president of OpenAI, makes a claim that should stop any CISO in their tracks: security spending should vastly outstrip software spending. In a world where software is becoming nearly free to write, the value has shifted to reliability and trust — and security infrastructure becomes the actual moat.

Brockman argues AI has now definitively proven it can generate new knowledge, not just retrieve it. The Navier-Stokes and other century-old math breakthroughs contain surprising insights that domain experts are still mining. "It's not just about the machine giving you an answer. It's really what can a human equipped with these tools achieve that was unthinkable before."

On drug discovery, he names it as the single most important AI application. His reasoning: neural nets have no inherent reason to be better at language than at biology. The real bottleneck won't be the AI — it'll be data availability and regulatory structure. He predicts AI will quickly unlock 80% of what's possible in drug development, with the remaining 20% gated on data innovation and wet lab advances.

He also introduced DOTS, OpenAI's proactive always-on personal agent. His own Dot monitors tasks he committed to but hasn't completed, catches calendar conflicts, and integrates inbox and Slack without being asked. It's not a capability change, he says — it's a form factor change: same frontier intelligence, always running in the background.

On cybersecurity, OpenAI has made a $1 billion commitment to help frontline defenders access their models. Internally, they paused 25 production engineers to security-harden their own infrastructure using their peak intelligence models. His take: defenders are currently in a window of opportunity — but that window is closing fast.
https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

Greg Brockman，OpenAI 联合创始人兼总裁，提出了一个值得所有 CISO 警醒的论断：安全支出应该远超软件支出。在软件编写成本趋近于零的世界里，价值已转移到可靠性和可信度上——安全基础设施才是真正的护城河。

Brockman 认为，AI 现在已明确证明它能产生新知识，而不只是检索已有知识。Navier-Stokes 等百年数学难题的突破中包含着领域专家至今仍在挖掘的惊人洞见。"这不只是让机器给你一个答案，而是一个配备了这些工具的人类，能够实现之前无法想象的成就。"

在药物研发方面，他将其列为 AI 最重要的应用领域，理由基于第一性原理：神经网络没有理由在语言上比在生物学上更擅长。真正的瓶颈不在 AI 本身，而在数据获取和监管结构。他预测 AI 将迅速解锁药物研发中 80% 的可能性，剩余 20% 有待数据创新和湿实验室突破。

他还介绍了 DOTS——OpenAI 的全天候主动运行个人 agent。他自己的 Dot 会追踪他承诺但未完成的任务、发现日历冲突、整合收件箱和 Slack，无需主动提示。他描述这不是能力上的变化，而是形态上的变化：同样的前沿智能，只是全程在后台运行。

在网络安全方面，OpenAI 承诺投入 10 亿美元帮助一线防御者访问其模型；内部也曾暂停 25 名生产工程师的日常工作，专门用顶级智能模型对自身基础设施进行安全加固。他对当下时机的判断：防御者目前处于一个机会窗口期——但这个窗口正在快速关闭。
