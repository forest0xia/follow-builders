**Latent Space — "Building Legal Agents When You Can't See the Data"**
*Guest: Jacob Lauritzen, CTO of Legora*
https://www.youtube.com/@LatentSpacePod

**The Takeaway:** Building AI agents for lawyers is uniquely hard — not because of the models, but because the data is so confidential you can never look at it. Legora has turned "flying blind" into a core engineering discipline.

Legora is one of the fastest-growing legal tech companies in history, having started in a Stockholm basement at YC W24 and now serving enterprise law firms across Europe and the US. Jacob Lauritzen, their CTO, walks through what it actually takes to ship AI agents in a domain where the data is triple-confidential — not just private to the law firm, but often confidential to their clients.

The core product insight is that legal AI isn't just a better search box. Legora built "Tabular Review" — a feature now so prevalent that even Anthropic's Claude for Legal has adopted the terminology — for bulk extraction and analysis of large contract sets during due diligence. Beyond doing the work, they've invested heavily in *orchestrating* the work: Lists (like Linear for legal tasks), Intake (client-lawyer collaboration), and Portal (lawyer-to-lawyer, client-to-lawyer, agent-to-agent workflows).

The data blindness problem is genuinely severe. Unlike most software companies, Legora cannot read their own users' traces or logs, because even paraphrased data risks exposing privileged client communications. Their solution: an evals team that builds synthetic benchmarks approximating what they *think* users are doing, classified into failure-mode buckets and task types. It's imperfect but it works.

On infrastructure, Legora built everything on Azure — not by preference, but because law firms in 2023 had already cleared Microsoft as a vendor (via Office 365 security reviews) and flatly refused AWS and Google. That constraint has largely dissolved; cloud is now normal in legal.

Lauritzen is cautiously optimistic about AI's impact on the legal profession: paralegals and junior lawyers may see significant displacement, but senior lawyers will become dramatically more productive. The partnership model itself may need to change to incentivize codifying institutional knowledge — the firms that do will accumulate durable IP in a historically human-capital-only industry.

**The Takeaway（中文）：** 为律师构建 AI agent 的最大难题不在于模型，而在于数据的高度机密性，使得团队根本无法查看用户的实际行为轨迹。Legora 将"在黑暗中飞行"转化成了一项核心工程能力。

Legora 是有史以来增长最快的法律科技公司之一，从斯德哥尔摩地下室起步，经过 YC W24 加速，如今已服务于欧美各大律所。CTO Jacob Lauritzen 深度讲述了在数据三重保密（公司保密+律所保密+客户保密）的法律领域构建 AI agent 的真实挑战。

核心产品洞察：法律 AI 不只是一个更好的搜索框。Legora 构建了 "Tabular Review"——一个用于在尽职调查中批量提取和分析大量合同的功能，影响力大到 Anthropic 的 Claude for Legal 也借用了这一术语。除了"做工作"，他们还重投资于"协调工作"：Lists（类似 Legal 版 Linear）、Intake（客户与律师协作）、Portal（律师对律师、客户对律师、agent对agent的工作流）。

数据盲目性问题极为严峻。与大多数软件公司不同，Legora 无法读取用户的任何操作记录，因为即使是经过脱敏的数据也可能泄露特权客户通信。他们的解决方案是：建立一支 evals 团队，构建合成基准数据集，模拟他们"猜测"用户正在做的事，并分类到失败模式桶和任务类型中。方案不够完美，但行之有效。

在基础设施选型上，Legora 选择了 Azure——并非偏好使然，而是因为2023年的律所已通过 Office 365 安全审核将微软列为可信供应商，对 AWS 和 Google 则一律拒之门外。如今这一限制已基本消失，云服务在法律行业已成常态。

Lauritzen 对 AI 对法律行业的冲击持谨慎乐观态度：助理律师和初级律师可能面临显著替代，但高级律师的生产力将大幅提升。合伙人制度本身可能需要调整激励结构，以鼓励将机构知识编码为系统资产——在这个历来只有人力资本的行业，率先实现知识资产化的律所，将拥有真正持久的竞争壁垒。
