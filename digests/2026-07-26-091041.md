**Anthropic Engineering** published a postmortem on recent Claude Code quality reports, tracing degraded responses to three separate changes made between March and April that affected Claude Code, the Claude Agent SDK, and Claude Cowork (the API was unaffected). The core issue: on March 4, Claude Code's default reasoning effort was quietly dropped from high to medium to reduce UI latency — the wrong tradeoff, reverted on April 7 after user feedback. **Anthropic Engineering** also published a deep-dive on how they're scaling Managed Agents by decoupling "the brain from the hands." The key insight: harnesses that wrap agent behavior inevitably encode assumptions about what models can't do — but those assumptions go stale as models improve. They found that context-reset logic added for Claude Sonnet 4.5 (which suffered "context anxiety" near its limit) became dead weight on Claude Opus 4.5, which had outgrown the problem. Managed Agents solves this by offering a stable, hosted interface that outlasts any specific harness implementation — so the infra layer can keep up with model progress without requiring constant rewrites.
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents

**Anthropic 工程团队**同时发布了一篇深度文章，探讨如何通过将"大脑与双手解耦"来扩展 Managed Agents。核心洞察是：包裹 agent 行为的 harness 必然编码了对模型能力局限的假设——但随着模型进步，这些假设会逐渐过时。他们发现，为解决 Claude Sonnet 4.5 在上下文接近上限时出现"上下文焦虑"而添加的重置逻辑，在 Claude Opus 4.5 上已成为多余的死代码，因为这个行为已经消失了。Managed Agents 通过提供一个稳定的、托管的接口来解决这个问题，使基础设施层能够跟上模型进步的步伐，无需不断重写 harness。
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents

## PODCASTS

**Unsupervised Learning, Episode 91: "Top AI Analyst Unpacks Today's AI Hype Cycle"**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vDY_ocrkQ5w

**The Takeaway:** Benedict Evans argues that the right way to think about AI isn't to debate whether it's bigger than the internet — it's to study what actually happened the last five times a world-changing platform shift arrived, and use that as a lens for where value will and won't accrue.

Jacob Efron of Redpoint Ventures sat down with Benedict Evans, one of the most analytically rigorous independent tech analysts writing today. Evans is known for his sweeping annual presentations on the state of tech, and this conversation covers the territory he's been mapping: how to reason about AI's impact when you can't benchmark it against clean historical precedent.

Evans's core move is a discipline of historical analogy — not to prove predictions, but to pressure-test assumptions. Mobile infrastructure, he points out, carried a trillion dollars of traffic growth over fifteen years and captured almost none of the value created. All of it went up the stack to Uber, YouTube, and banks. Foundation models could follow the same pattern: massive capex, intense competition, commoditizing infrastructure while value accrues to applications above. He's skeptical of the "we'll be like Windows" framing from OpenAI, noting that LLMs lack the network effects that made Windows sticky.

On AGI hype, Evans is characteristically blunt: if superintelligence actually arrives, worrying about middle-class unemployment becomes moot. That's a binary you can set aside and focus on what's actually happening in enterprise software adoption today — which he describes as a "jagged edge" where capabilities exist but real-world reliability and integration remain uneven.

His read on the current consumer AI moment echoes what many builders see: it's like the mobile internet in 2002. Obviously transformative, often broken, and the killer apps haven't shown up yet. "Meanwhile, let's build some stuff and work out what people do with it" is his conclusion — and probably the most useful framing for anyone trying to ship products into this wave.

**Unsupervised Learning 第 91 期：《顶级 AI 分析师深度解析今日 AI 炒作周期》**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vDY_ocrkQ5w

**核心结论：** Benedict Evans 认为，思考 AI 的正确方式不是争论它是否比互联网更重大——而是研究过去五次颠覆性平台迁移真正发生了什么，并以此为视角分析价值将在哪里积累、在哪里消散。

Redpoint Ventures 的 Jacob Efron 与 Benedict Evans 展开了一次深度对话。Evans 是当今最具分析严谨性的独立科技分析师之一，以每年发布的全面科技全景报告而闻名。这次对话聚焦于他一直在描绘的议题：当 AI 无法与干净的历史先例对标时，如何理性评估其影响。

Evans 的核心方法是历史类比的纪律——不是为了证明预测，而是为了检验假设。他指出，移动基础设施在过去十五年中承载了万亿美元量级的流量增长，却几乎没有捕获任何由此产生的价值——所有价值都流向了上游的 Uber、YouTube 和银行。基础模型可能遵循同样的规律：大量资本支出、激烈竞争、基础设施商品化，而价值在应用层积累。他对 OpenAI "我们会像 Windows 一样"的定位持怀疑态度，指出大语言模型缺乏让 Windows 保持粘性的网络效应。

在 AGI 炒作问题上，Evans 一如既往地直率：如果超级智能真的到来，担忧中产阶级失业就变得毫无意义。这是一个可以搁置的二元问题，不如把精力放在今天企业软件采用中真实发生的事情上——他将其描述为"锯齿状边缘"，能力已经存在，但现实世界的可靠性和集成仍参差不齐。

他对当前消费端 AI 状态的判断，与很多产品构建者的观察不谋而合：这像极了 2002 年的移动互联网——显然将改变一切，但经常出故障，杀手级应用还没有出现。"与此同时，让我们构建一些东西，看看人们拿它做什么"——这是他的结论，也可能是当下任何试图在这波浪潮中交付产品的人，最有用的思维框架。
