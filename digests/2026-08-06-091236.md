## PODCASTS

**AI & I by Every — "Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social"**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlI-5W7d7uU

**The Takeaway:** The AI consumer product wave is still in its "deeply technical founder" phase — and the multiplayer, social, network-effect layer that will define the next winner hasn't been built yet.

Dan Shipper hosts Sarah Tavel, partner at Benchmark and former early employee at Pinterest, for a wide-ranging conversation on what consumer technology history can teach us about where AI is headed. Tavel maps the arc of tech paradigm shifts: Google was 95% backend engineering genius; Facebook was technical, but less so; Instagram and Pinterest were led by product geniuses with almost no deep technical background. Her thesis: as underlying infrastructure matures, the center of gravity shifts from technical depth to product intuition.

Where does AI sit today? Early. ChatGPT and Character AI are, in Tavel's framing, still primarily backend-driven products — the model is the product. She argues that the missing piece isn't better models, it's the social layer: a UGC community where the people who are really good at using AI make it easier for everyone else, with status mechanics that incentivize contribution. Think: the person who makes the perfect system prompt becomes a celebrity in the network.

Tavel also calls out a real friction she experiences as a power user: "It shouldn't be this hard." The need to carefully engineer custom instructions just to get useful responses is a symptom of a product that hasn't yet been designed for non-technical users. DeepSeek, she speculates, may mark a turning point — the moment where infrastructure costs drop enough to make rich product experiences viable.

The conversation closes on the tantalizing open question: who will build the multiplayer, status-seeking, socially engaging layer on top of AI? It's not ChatGPT. It's probably not anyone who's launched yet.

**AI & I by Every — "下一个 AI 爆款产品将是社交产品"**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlI-5W7d7uU

**核心洞察：** AI 消费产品浪潮仍处于"深度技术型创始人"主导阶段——那个能定义下一代赢家的多人、社交、网络效应层，还没有被构建出来。

Dan Shipper 邀请 Benchmark 合伙人、Pinterest 早期员工 Sarah Tavel，围绕消费技术历史能为 AI 时代带来哪些启示展开深度对话。Tavel 梳理了技术范式转移的规律：Google 是 95% 的后端工程天才；Facebook 技术过硬，但不如 Google；Instagram 和 Pinterest 则由几乎没有深厚技术背景的产品天才领导。她的核心论点：随着底层基础设施趋于成熟，竞争重心从技术深度向产品直觉转移。

AI 现在处于哪个阶段？还早。在 Tavel 看来，ChatGPT 和 Character AI 仍然主要是"后端驱动型"产品——模型就是产品。她认为，缺失的不是更好的模型，而是社交层：一个 UGC 社区，让真正擅长使用 AI 的人帮助其他人，并通过地位机制激励贡献。设想一下：那个写出完美 system prompt 的人，可以成为整个网络里的"名人"。

Tavel 也直言自己作为深度用户的真实痛点："这不应该这么难。"需要精心设计 custom instruction 才能得到有用的回应，这本身就说明产品还没有为非技术用户设计。她猜测，DeepSeek 或许是一个转折点——基础设施成本的下降，可能让丰富的产品体验真正变得可行。

对话最终以一个令人悬念的开放式问题收尾：谁会在 AI 之上构建那个多人参与、追求地位、充满社交感的体验层？不是 ChatGPT。也很可能不是任何已经发布的产品。
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlI-5W7d7uU
