**Training Data — "A Model For Everyone: How Ronak From Trajectory Is Building Products For Continual Learning"**

**The Takeaway:** Ronak, co-founder and CEO of Trajectory, is building the missing infrastructure layer between frontier AI research and the real world — a platform that lets any company train, evaluate, and deploy customized AI models without a massive research team.

Ronak spent time at Windsurf (now Codeium) training frontier coding models in production, alongside co-founders who worked at DeepMind on Gemini Robotics and at Apple on the Vision Pro. The common thread across all three experiences: massive research infrastructure being built over and over again, in isolation, to solve the same fundamental problem — translating real-world feedback into model improvements. That's the gap Trajectory is filling.

The core thesis is that we are living in an era of "pseudo continual learning." Today's AI agents are intelligent but static: they process a task and return a result, but they don't accumulate experience or update from it. Ronak's analogy is vivid — today's agents are like MapQuest, offline and batch-updated, while the goal is Google Maps, where every route driven improves the system in real time.

Trajectory's platform lets AI-native companies bring their production agent traces, define what "good" looks like, and train a customized model in one click. The results are concrete: Harvey (legal AI) trained a model that jumped from 4% to 18% accuracy on their own benchmark at six times lower cost than the frontier model. Rogo (finance AI) trained a model optimized for conciseness and token efficiency — dramatically reducing cost while improving on the tasks they care about.

> "I want to basically take that 1% and turn that into 99% so that we are actually learning from all of this built up experience that lives in the world right now."

The research frontier Ronak is pushing toward is quantifying curiosity in AI agents — giving them a mechanism to recognize what they don't know and actively seek to learn it. He distinguishes this sharply from Recursive Self Improvement (RSI): RSI puts a model in a black box to optimize itself, which raises alignment risks. Continual learning, by contrast, keeps humans permanently in the loop — the model only improves by interacting with people and getting better at the tasks they want done.

His long-term vision draws on Adam Smith's *Wealth of Nations*: the current AI landscape is pre-industrial, where every company consults the same general-purpose oracle. The future is specialization — every company trains models tuned to their own workflows, moving AI adoption from the current 1% of enterprises to something approaching 99%.

**要点一句话：** Trajectory 联合创始人兼 CEO Ronak 正在构建 frontier AI 研究与真实世界之间缺失的基础设施层——一个让任何公司都能在没有大型研究团队的前提下，完成 AI 模型训练、评估和部署的平台。

Ronak 曾在 Windsurf（现 Codeium）参与生产环境中的 frontier 编程模型训练；他的联合创始人则分别来自 DeepMind（Gemini Robotics 项目）和 Apple（Vision Pro 团队）。三段经历背后有一条共同的线索：同样的基础问题——如何将真实世界的反馈转化为模型改进——却在不同公司各自封闭地重复建造。这正是 Trajectory 要填补的空白。

Trajectory 的核心命题是：我们正处于"伪持续学习"时代。当今的 AI agent 聪明但静态——处理任务、返回结果，却不积累经验、不从中更新。Ronak 打了一个生动的比喻：今天的 agent 更像 MapQuest，离线运行、批量更新；而真正的目标是 Google Maps——每一次导航都在实时优化整个系统。

Trajectory 的平台允许 AI 原生公司将生产环境中的 agent trace 和"什么是好结果"的定义带进来，一键完成定制模型训练。效果是具体可见的：Harvey（法律 AI）用该平台训练了一个模型，在自建 benchmark 上准确率从 4% 跳升至 18%，成本仅为 frontier 模型的六分之一；Rogo（金融 AI）训练的模型在简洁性和 token 效率上显著提升，成本大幅降低，同时在核心任务上表现更好。

> "我想把那 1% 变成 99%，让我们真正从这个世界中积累的所有经验里学习。"

Ronak 正在探索的研究前沿是为 AI agent 量化"好奇心"——赋予它们识别自身知识盲区并主动探索的机制。他将这一路径与 RSI（递归自我改进）明确区分：RSI 是将模型放进黑箱让其自我优化，存在对齐风险；而持续学习始终将人类保持在循环之中，模型只通过与人互动、完成人类设定的任务来进步。

他的长期愿景引用了亚当·斯密的《国富论》：当前的 AI 格局仍处于前工业时代，每家公司都在咨询同一个通用 oracle。未来是专业化分工——每家公司训练专属于自身工作流的模型，AI 在企业中的渗透率将从当前的 1% 迈向 99%。
