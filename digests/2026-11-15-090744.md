**Unsupervised Learning — Ep 97: Jev Creator on Decision Models, Benchmark Fatigue & Why the Labs Missed It**

**The Takeaway:** The next frontier isn't smarter models — it's building AI as a *reliable*, *composable* primitive, like SQL rather than a black box that leaks failure modes into every product built on top of it.

Jogo, CEO of TypeSave and creator of Jev — an AI decision model that went viral in the developer community — joined Jacob Efron on Unsupervised Learning roughly a month after launch, with enough distance to reflect on what actually happened. His core argument: the AI industry has been building on the wrong foundation. When every application encodes assumptions about a specific model's behavior — its quirks, its strengths, its tendencies — you've built the AI equivalent of a monolith. That's not a product. That's technical debt at scale.

Jogo's north star is provider agnosticism. He wants Jev to be the SQL of intelligent systems: so reliable you don't even think about which underlying model is running. This is a pointed critique of the current ecosystem, where, as he puts it, "people start building theories of mind of the submodels they call."

He also pushes back hard on benchmark hype. His framing: intelligence isn't a single axis. Even the best AI systems still require humans in the loop for real-world production use, despite "superhuman" scores on code and math. The honest thing to do is communicate those limitations clearly — which is why he calls Jev a "system one" model, referencing the fast-but-fallible cognitive mode from behavioral psychology.

On Anthropic's upcoming IPO, he's characteristically direct: "The 'let them cure cancer' is going to be like Marie Antoinette's 'let them eat cake.'" Some investors, he argues, are pricing in a future that misunderstands what AI can actually do — and that miscalibration will show.

His closing message is a call to arms for builders: "Start really building again... we need to start untraining ourselves of this lack of agency that LLMs have taught us."
https://www.youtube.com/@RedpointAI

**要点：** 下一个前沿不是更智能的模型，而是将 AI 打造成像 SQL 一样**可靠、可组合**的基础原语——而非一个将故障模式渗透进每个上层产品的黑盒子。

TypeSave CEO、Jev 创造者 Jogo 在 Jev 病毒式传播约一个月后，登上了 Jacob Efron 主持的 Unsupervised Learning 播客，以更从容的视角回顾了这一切究竟发生了什么。他的核心论断：AI 行业一直在错误的基础上构建产品。当每一个应用都把对某个特定模型行为的假设——它的怪癖、它的优势、它的倾向——编码进去，你实际上制造的是 AI 版单体架构。那不是产品，是规模化的技术债。

Jogo 的北极星是提供商无关性。他希望 Jev 成为智能系统的 SQL：可靠到你根本不需要考虑底层跑的是哪个模型。这是对当前生态系统的直接批评——正如他所说，"人们开始构建他们所调用的子模型的心智模型"。

他还强烈反对 benchmark 炒作。他的框架：智能不是单一维度的。即便是最好的 AI 系统，在真实生产环境中仍然需要人在循环中，尽管在代码和数学上取得了"超人"的分数。诚实的做法是清晰地传达这些局限性——这也是他将 Jev 称为"system one"模型的原因，借用行为心理学中快速但易犯错的认知模式。

谈到 Anthropic 即将到来的 IPO，他一如既往地直率："'让他们治愈癌症'将成为 Marie Antoinette 的'让他们吃蛋糕'。"他认为，部分投资者的定价基于对 AI 真实能力的误解，这种错误校准终将显现。

他的结束语是对建造者的一声号召："重新开始真正的建造……我们需要开始摆脱 LLM 教给我们的那种缺乏主体性的思维方式。"
