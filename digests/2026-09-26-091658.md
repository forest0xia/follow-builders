**Claude Blog: Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude**

Anthropic announced a major product unification: Claude Cowork and Claude chat are merging into a single Claude experience, rolling out first to Pro and Max plans. Alongside the merge, three new tools are launching in beta — Claude Docs (collaborative document editing), Claude Slides (AI-drafted presentations downloadable as PowerPoint or PDF), and Claude Design (visual design, now available from any conversation). The core motivation: users found it frustrating to decide whether a task belonged in Cowork or chat, and work started in one didn't carry over to the other. Now Claude can determine what a task requires — long-running background work, a document, a presentation — all from the same conversation. Enterprises can schedule recurring tasks, and Claude starts without being asked. "I could have Claude pull up my legal research database, and it would pull all the cases, read them, figure out which other cases I might need, download them, and store them in a folder for my personal review." — Andrew Keller, Senior Economist.

https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude

Anthropic 宣布了一项重大产品整合：Claude Cowork 与 Claude 聊天界面将合并为统一的 Claude 体验，首先向 Pro 和 Max 计划用户推出。与此同时，三款新工具进入 beta 阶段——Claude Docs（协作文档编辑）、Claude Slides（AI 起草演示文稿，可导出为 PowerPoint 或 PDF），以及 Claude Design（视觉设计，现可从任意对话中调用）。此次整合的核心动因是：用户常常苦于判断一项任务该交给 Cowork 还是聊天界面，且在一个地方开始的工作无法延续到另一个地方。如今 Claude 能够自行判断任务需求——无论是长时间后台运行、撰写文档还是制作演示文稿——都可以在同一个对话中完成。企业可以设置定期任务，Claude 会自动启动，无需每次提醒。"我可以让 Claude 调取我的法律研究数据库，它会提取所有相关案例、逐一阅读、判断还需要哪些补充案例，下载后整理到文件夹供我审阅。"——高级经济学家 Andrew Keller。

**No Priors: "Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era with Sequence Holdings Co-Founder and CEO Michael Lee"**

**The Takeaway:** The next massive AI value-creation opportunity isn't building new software — it's buying entrenched incumbents and redesigning their organizations from the ground up for an AI-native future.

Michael Lee co-founded Sequence Holdings twenty months ago with a thesis that runs counter to most venture thinking: the companies best positioned to benefit from AI aren't startups, but established incumbents in industries like insurance brokerage and banking — precisely because they have moats (brand, regulatory, network effects) that startups can't easily replicate. Lee came to this view as an investor who covered AI as early as 2017 at Lone Pine Capital, witnessing the shift from convolutional nets and LSTMs to the transformer era.

Sequence's model is structurally different from traditional private equity. It's a permanent holding company — not a fund — that buys controlling stakes and then "refounds" its portfolio companies using frontier engineering. There's no deployment cadence, no LP pressure to exit in three years. The logic: genuine AI transformation requires long-term operational commitment that fund structures inherently work against.

Lee's critique of the alternatives is pointed. Vendors optimize for wallet share, not transformation. Software tools are built for existing workflows and can't reorganize a business around what AI makes possible. And traditional PE firms celebrate the investor, not the engineer — which makes it structurally difficult to attract the engineering talent required.

The firm's first investment, BankSouth in Georgia, offers early evidence for the thesis. In six months post-investment, Sequence reduced average consumer loan underwriting time by 94%. Commercial loan processing dropped from 30 days to 11. When loan volume doubled in Q2, the bank absorbed it with a smaller underwriting team than before — not through layoffs, but by moving people to higher-value work.

The second bet is bigger: Baldwin Insurance, a $7.7 billion take-private backed by the Dell family office — the largest AI take-private to date. Insurance brokerage fits Sequence's criteria: a $2 trillion market in annual premiums, high gross retention (90%+), and structural advantages that make it nearly impossible for startups to compete on price or relationships.

"Every company on the planet has a celebrated persona... In a world where you believe that alpha comes from engineering and AI, you need to create a culture whereby the celebrated persona is the engineer. And that's what's required."

https://www.youtube.com/@NoPriorsPodcast

**要点：** 下一个 AI 领域的巨大价值创造机会，不是从头构建新软件——而是收购根深蒂固的成熟企业，并从底层将其组织架构重塑为 AI 原生模式。

Sequence Holdings 联合创始人兼 CEO Michael Lee 在二十个月前创立了这家公司，其核心论断与大多数风险投资的思维逻辑背道而驰：最有条件从 AI 中获益的，不是初创公司，而是保险经纪和银行业等领域的成熟龙头——恰恰是因为它们拥有初创公司难以复制的护城河（品牌、监管壁垒、网络效应）。Lee 早在 2017 年便以投资人身份开始研究 AI，亲历了从卷积网络、LSTM 到 Transformer 时代的演变，由此形成了这一判断。

Sequence 的模式与传统私募股权有本质区别。它是一家永久持股公司，而非基金——通过收购控股权后，利用前沿工程能力对被投企业进行"重建"。没有部署节奏，没有 LP 三年退出的压力。其内在逻辑是：真正的 AI 转型需要长期运营投入，而基金结构天然与这一要求相悖。

Lee 对其他路径的批评直接而有力：服务商优化的是钱包份额，而非变革；软件工具是为现有工作流设计的，无法重组围绕 AI 可能性构建的业务；传统 PE 机构的文化崇拜的是投资人而非工程师，这从结构上就难以吸引 AI 转型所需的顶尖工程人才。

Sequence 的第一笔投资——乔治亚州的 BankSouth——已初步印证了这一论断。投资后六个月内，Sequence 将消费贷款平均审批时间缩短了 94%，商业贷款处理周期从 30 天压缩至 11 天。第二季度贷款量翻倍时，银行用比以前更小的团队消化了所有业务——不是靠裁员，而是将人力转移到更高价值的工作上。

第二笔投资规模更大：以 77 亿美元将 Baldwin Insurance 私有化，由 Dell 家族办公室联合支持——这是迄今为止最大规模的 AI 私有化交易。保险经纪业符合 Sequence 的选择标准：每年超过 2 万亿美元的保费市场、90% 以上的客户留存率，以及让初创公司在价格和关系上几乎无法竞争的结构性优势。
