**Latent Space: Scaling Linear's GTM to $100M ARR — Cristina Cordova, COO**

The Takeaway: The best GTM leaders earn their role by being indispensable at the functions the founders never learned.

Cristina Cordova, COO of Linear, ran the full playbook for scaling a product-led growth company from "beloved by startups" to a $100M ARR business serving frontier AI labs and enterprise teams — and the story is less about hiring salespeople and more about building a category.

When Cordova joined Linear three and a half years ago (from Notion, where she built out their platform, partnerships, and self-serve growth, and before that Stripe, where she spent seven and a half years), Linear was essentially capped at mid-market. Most customers had never spoken to a human at the company. That worked well until it didn't. The goal: sell to companies that require contracts, pilots, security reviews, and procurement processes — and 10x revenue from there.

Her onboarding trial was essentially the job itself: come up with the go-to-market plan for Linear for the next 6-12 months. If they didn't like what she produced, they shouldn't hire her. The brand strategy she landed on: "Make Linear inevitable." Either you're already on it, or you're planning your move.

What's changed since: Linear's CEO posted that "issue tracking is dead," and customers largely agreed. The company is repositioning as a product development system — one where agents do the manual work, humans set direction. Features like Triage Intelligence automatically label, assign, and route bug reports and feature requests without human intervention. The pivot is working because the company is "not so big to kill itself" — and their customers, including most of the top frontier AI labs, are already operating this way.

Her counterintuitive take on COOs: not every company needs one. Hire someone like her only when there are functions the founding team can't evaluate themselves — not simply because the business is growing.

**Latent Space：将 Linear 的 GTM 规模化至 1 亿美元年收入 — Cristina Cordova，COO**

核心洞察：最好的 GTM 负责人，是靠在创始人从未掌握的领域变得不可或缺来赢得这个位置的。

Linear COO Cristina Cordova 分享了将一家产品驱动型公司从"初创公司的最爱"扩展为年收入 1 亿美元、服务前沿 AI 实验室和企业客户的全套打法——这个故事的核心不是招销售，而是创造品类。

Cordova 三年半前加入 Linear（此前在 Notion 负责平台、合作伙伴关系和自增长，再之前在 Stripe 待了七年半）时，Linear 基本上卡在中端市场上限。大多数客户从未和公司任何人说过话。这个模式一直有效，直到它不再有效。目标：开始赢得那些需要合同、试点、安全审查和采购流程的企业客户——并从那里实现 10 倍增长。

她的入职考核就是这份工作本身：为 Linear 未来 6 到 12 个月制定 GTM 计划。如果结果不令人满意，就不应该雇用她。她最终确定的品牌策略是：让 Linear 成为必然。要么你已经在用了，要么你正在计划迁移过来。

此后发生的变化：Linear CEO 发帖称"issue tracking 已死"，大多数客户表示认同。公司正在重新定位为产品开发系统——agent 负责手动工作，人类负责定方向。像 Triage Intelligence 这样的功能可以自动对 bug 报告和需求进行标注、分配和分流，无需人工干预。这次转型之所以奏效，是因为公司"还没大到会把自己逼死"——而他们的客户，包括大多数顶级前沿 AI 实验室，已经在这样运作了。

她对 COO 角色的反直觉观点：不是每家公司都需要 COO。只有当创始团队无法评估某些职能是否做得好时，才需要招一个像她这样的人——而不是单纯因为业务在增长。
