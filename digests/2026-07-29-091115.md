**Thibault Sottiaux**

Thibault Sottiaux, who works on Codex and ChatGPT at OpenAI, published a major transparency update on GPT-5.6 Sol's usage limits — a post that drew nearly 12,000 likes. He reset usage limits for all ChatGPT Work and Codex users, explaining that Sol is a far more ambitious worker than previous models: it makes more tool calls, runs longer, coordinates complex multi-agent workflows, and runs tool calls in parallel via "code mode." This led to usage draining faster than expected, especially for power users. OpenAI has shipped several efficiency improvements and expects typical usage to last ~18% longer. He was candid: "Capability and efficiency do not always improve at the same pace, and some issues only become clear once people are using the model at real-world scale."
https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2082317452755751098

Thibault Sottiaux 负责 OpenAI 的 Codex 和 ChatGPT 业务，他发布了一篇关于 GPT-5.6 Sol 使用配额的重大透明度说明，获得近 12,000 个赞。他为所有 ChatGPT Work 和 Codex 用户重置了使用上限，并解释道：Sol 比以往模型更"勤奋"——它会发起更多工具调用、处理更长任务、协调复杂的多智能体工作流，并通过"code mode"并行执行工具调用。这导致部分用户（尤其是重度用户）的额度消耗远超预期。OpenAI 已推出多项效率优化，预计典型场景下使用时长将延长约 18%。他坦言："能力与效率的提升并不总是同步进行，有些问题只有在真实规模下使用后才会浮现。"
