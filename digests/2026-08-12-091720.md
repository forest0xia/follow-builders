### Boris Cherny · Claude Code @Anthropic

Boris Cherny from Anthropic's Claude Code team made an important observation about the evolving nature of LLM-generated bugs. The types of coding errors AI models produce have shifted: instead of low-level off-by-one errors, bugs now tend to cluster around system design, UI usability, and missing broader context. As certain categories of coding have effectively been "solved," **adversarial code review** has emerged as a powerful countermeasure — even something as simple as a one-line prompt asking Claude to dynamically test every edge case in an iOS simulator. Claude Code's built-in `/code-review` skill supports different levels of rigor (low, medium, and beyond).

https://x.com/bcherny/status/2087284684103537011

Anthropic Claude Code 团队的 Boris Cherny 对 LLM 生成代码中 bug 性质的演变提出了重要观察。AI 模型产生的编码错误类型已经转变：相较于低级的"差一错误"，bug 现在更多集中在系统设计、UI 可用性和缺乏更广泛上下文等层面。随着某些类别的编码问题已被"攻克"，**对抗性代码审查**成为了强有力的应对手段——哪怕只是一条让 Claude 在 iOS 模拟器中动态测试所有边缘情况的单行提示词。Claude Code 内置的 `/code-review` 技能支持不同严格程度（低、中等等）。

### Thariq · Claude Code @Anthropic

The watermarking is invisible to users but detectable by tools — for example, you could verify whether a pull request was generated by Claude Code. Thariq noted the system has known limitations, with more details available in Anthropic's help center.

https://x.com/trq212/status/2087258090169414008

### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "The Biggest AI Deployment Nobody Talks About | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas"

**The Takeaway:** Samsara — the $20 billion company running what may be the largest AI deployment in the physical world — has built a data moat so deep that even frontier AI labs can't replicate it: 25 trillion data points a year from millions of vehicles traversing 99% of US roads, every single day.

Matt Turck's conversation with Samsara cofounder and CEO Sanjit Biswas reframes what "AI at scale" actually means. While the industry fixates on tokens and software agents, Samsara is quietly doing something more fundamental: digitizing the physical world. From GPS tracking on millions of trucks to in-cab cameras monitoring driver fatigue, Samsara's platform has prevented an estimated **380,000 road accidents in the past year** — a number Biswas admits "feels abstract even to me."

The episode gets genuinely fascinating when Biswas explains why this data is irreplaceable: "These are not tokens you're gonna find online. You can't crawl Reddit and find out what happened on a construction site." That proprietary data advantage — built through years of physical sensor deployment — gives Samsara's AI a defensibility that pure software plays simply don't have.

On generative AI, Biswas is specific and concrete. Vision-language models now let Samsara analyze video clips to distinguish a driver who slammed the brakes in a panic from one who was dodging a deer — and deliver the right feedback to each. Generative video enables AI-generated coaching avatars that can resemble a company's VP of Safety, delivering personalized end-of-week driver coaching at a scale no human team could manage.

The conversation closes on the infrastructure boom: the US electrical grid is set to triple capacity in five years, and there simply aren't enough electricians, truck drivers, or tradespeople to build it. AI won't replace these workers — it'll make each one dramatically more productive.

**MAD Podcast 与 Matt Turck — "无人谈及的最大 AI 部署 | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas"**

**核心洞见：** Samsara——这家市值 200 亿美元的公司，正在运营全球规模最大的物理世界 AI 部署——构建了一条深不可及的数据护城河：每年来自数百万辆车辆的 25 万亿个数据点，每天覆盖 99% 的美国道路，任何前沿 AI 实验室都无法复制。

Matt Turck 与 Samsara 联合创始人兼 CEO Sanjit Biswas 的对话，重新定义了"规模化 AI"的真实含义。当行业目光聚焦于 token 和软件 agent 时，Samsara 正在默默完成一件更根本的事：将物理世界数字化。从数百万辆卡车上的 GPS 追踪，到监测司机疲劳状态的驾驶舱摄像头，Samsara 的平台去年估计**预防了 38 万起道路事故**——Biswas 坦言这个数字"即使对我自己来说也感觉很抽象"。

当 Biswas 解释为何这些数据不可替代时，访谈变得尤为精彩："这些不是你能在网上找到的 token。你没法从 Reddit 上抓取施工现场发生了什么。"这种通过多年物理传感器部署积累的专有数据优势，让 Samsara 的 AI 具备了纯软件公司所没有的防御性壁垒。

在生成式 AI 方面，Biswas 给出了具体而实际的案例。视觉语言模型（VLM）现在能够分析视频片段，区分司机因恍神而急刹车与为躲避动物紧急制动的不同情景，并给出相应的针对性反馈。生成式视频甚至能创建 AI 生成的训练化身，模拟公司安全副总裁的形象，以任何人工团队都无法实现的规模向司机进行每周个性化驾驶辅导。

对话以基础设施热潮收尾：未来五年，美国电网容量将三倍扩张，但电工、卡车司机和蓝领工人根本不够用。AI 不会取代这些工人——而是会让每个人的生产力大幅提升。
