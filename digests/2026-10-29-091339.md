**AI & I by Every — "Can You Use AI To Make Good Art? — With Fred Graver"**

**The Takeaway:** AI can amplify a creative voice, but it cannot give you one — and if you haven't first emptied out everything you know onto the page, you'll produce AI slop instead of art.

Fred Graver is a four-time Emmy Award-winning filmmaker and technologist whose career has spanned National Lampoon, Late Night with David Letterman, VH1 and MTV, Twitter's early celebrity strategy, and Microsoft AI. Now he's building compound production tools to help the next generation of storytellers use AI in their craft.

Graver breaks the creative process into three phases — ideation and design, production, and distribution — and argues that AI is already transforming all three. But his most counterintuitive point is what happens before any of it: creators must spend time away from AI, writing or recording everything they know about what they want to make, before opening a model. "Empty out. You will never have that opportunity again once you open AI. There's one chance to kind of be yourself and confront the idea."

His method for avoiding AI slop: build a "who I am as a writer" document, then a document about the specific project, then use AI as a virtual writer's room — not to generate ideas, but to pressure-test them. Crucially, he asks AI to tell him whether his idea sounds like something he would actually work on, whether there's a real audience for it, and what he still lacks to bring it to screen. "It can help you say it. There are so many ways to help you say it. But if you ask it to tell you what it is you should be saying, you'll get something that goes to the mean."

One structural critique stands out: Hollywood's adoption of AI is wildly uneven. Marketing teams are already A/B testing trailers nearly hour by hour using AI, while writers' rooms are just beginning to explore it. And distribution — Kevin Kelly's "1,000 true fans" model — has never been properly built for independent storytellers. That, Graver argues, is the real missing infrastructure for the next generation of creators.

**播客：AI & I by Every — 《你能用 AI 创作出好的艺术吗？》**

**核心洞见：** AI 可以放大创作者的声音，却无法替你拥有一个——如果你没有先在纸上倾尽所知，你只会生产 AI 糟粕，而非艺术。

Fred Graver 是四届艾美奖得主兼技术达人，职业生涯横跨《国家灯笼报》、《大卫·莱特曼深夜秀》、VH1 和 MTV、Twitter 早期明星战略，以及微软 AI 团队。如今他正在构建 compound 制作工具，帮助下一代讲故事的人将 AI 融入创作。

Graver 将创作流程分为三个阶段——创意与设计、制作、发行——并认为 AI 正在重塑每一个阶段。但他最反直觉的观点，是关于这一切开始之前的事：创作者必须先远离 AI，将自己对想要创作内容的一切所知用文字或语音记录下来，然后才能打开模型。"清空自己。一旦你打开 AI，你就再也没有这个机会了。这是你以真正的自己直面创意的唯一机会。"

他避免 AI 糟粕的方法：先写一份"我是怎样的写作者"文档，再写一份关于当前项目的文档，然后用 AI 作为虚拟编剧室——不是让它生成创意，而是用它来检验创意。关键是，他会问 AI：这个想法是否像是他会做的事、是否有真实受众、以及他还缺少什么才能把它搬上屏幕。"它能帮你表达。它有很多方式帮你表达。但如果你让它告诉你该说什么，你得到的将是平均值。"

有一个结构性批判尤为突出：好莱坞对 AI 的采纳极度不均衡。营销团队已经在几乎按小时用 AI 对预告片进行 A/B 测试，而编剧室才刚刚开始探索。而发行端——Kevin Kelly 的"1000 个铁杆粉丝"模型——从未真正为独立创作者搭建起来。Graver 认为，这才是下一代创作者真正缺失的基础设施。
