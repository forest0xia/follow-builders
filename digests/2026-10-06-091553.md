**Guillermo Rauch** (CEO of Vercel)

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch introduced gdp-ts — a library, linter, and AI skill that brings "Ghosts of Departed Proofs" from Haskell research into TypeScript. The idea: sensitive API functions require callers to present typed "proofs" that authorization checks were performed, verified by the TypeScript compiler at build time. The motivation is urgent: "Agents are writing more code than we can review, and they thrive in tight loops with hard constraints." A real-world example from the Vercel codebase: changing a Project's password requires proof of a specific role and entitlement.
https://x.com/rauchg/status/2107119811444748555

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 发布了 gdp-ts——一个将 Haskell 研究中的"Ghosts of Departed Proofs"理念引入 TypeScript 的库、linter 和 AI skill。核心思路：高权限 API 函数要求调用方提供类型化的"证明"，以确认其执行过授权检查，这一验证由 TypeScript 编译器在构建时完成。动机很迫切："agent 写的代码已经超出了我们的 review 能力，而它们在有明确约束的紧密循环中反而表现更好。" Vercel 内部的实际案例：修改项目密码，必须同时提供特定角色和权限的证明。
