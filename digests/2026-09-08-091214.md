**AI & I by Every — "A $10B Hedge Fund's AI Playbook (Best of the Pod)"**

**The Takeaway:** The most compelling AI transformation story right now isn't at a Silicon Valley startup — it's at a $10 billion hedge fund led by a CEO who made AI training mandatory for all 400 employees and wrote a firm-wide email declaring: "Not using these tools is like refusing to use the internet in 1995 because it wasn't perfect."

Will, CEO and CIO of Walleye Capital, is a Princeton engineer and Oxford math PhD who built his career writing algorithmic trading code. He's not a tech tourist — he's a career quant who sees generative AI as the next evolutionary step in a journey his firm has been on for a decade. Walleye runs substantial quantitative trading strategies that have used advanced statistics and AI for years, and now Will is extending that same rigor to every department, every employee.

The centerpiece of Walleye's internal AI stack is "Current" — a product that synthesizes analyst notes, broker PDFs, earnings transcripts, and any germane data for fundamental stock pickers. Over 50 competitor firms have requested beta access. Beyond Current, 75% of Walleye employees actively use AI tools weekly, and roughly a third use AI coding tools like Windsurf. Will's approach to change management is unusually direct: he wrote a firm-wide memo that began, "I use ChatGPT to write this email. You should be using it too and be proud of it... Using ChatGPT is not cheating. That's a non applicable idea from academia."

On the perennial fear that AI replaces workers: Will reaches for an Excel analogy. "If you're a long short stock picker and you can't use Excel to create a company model, you're totally obsolete." AI is the same inflection point, just faster. The tools don't eliminate thinking — they give you more time to think: "These tools don't negate the necessity to think. If anything, they should just give you more time to think." He also journals daily with AI assistance, converting bullet points into prose in under a minute — a task that used to take hours.

His longer-term vision — which he calls "the Borg," a nod to Star Trek — is an organization-wide intelligence layer where every Zoom recording, email, Slack message, and document flows continuously into LLMs for real-time synthesis and prediction. It's early, but Walleye's internal "Current" product is a working proof of concept. The same principles, he argues, apply to any organization willing to do the data infrastructure work.

**核心洞察：** 目前最引人注目的 AI 转型故事，不在硅谷初创公司，而在一家管理规模近 100 亿美元的对冲基金——其 CEO 将 AI 培训定为全体 400 名员工的必修课，并亲自发布全员邮件宣告："不使用这些工具，就像 1995 年因为互联网还不完美而拒绝使用互联网一样。"

Will 是 Walleye Capital 的 CEO 兼 CIO，普林斯顿工程学本科、牛津数学博士，职业生涯从编写算法交易代码起步。他不是技术圈的外来者，而是一位资深量化从业者，将生成式 AI 视为公司过去十年持续旅程的下一个进化节点。Walleye 长期运行着大规模量化交易策略，多年来一直深度运用先进统计学和 AI——如今，Will 正将同样的严谨标准推广到每一个部门、每一位员工。

Walleye 内部 AI 体系的核心是"Current"——一款整合分析师笔记、券商 PDF、财报记录等所有相关数据的产品，专为基本面股票投资者服务。已有超过 50 家竞争对手主动申请测试资格。在 Current 之外，75% 的 Walleye 员工每周活跃使用 AI 工具，约三分之一的员工使用 AI 编程工具（如 Windsurf）。Will 的变革管理方式出乎意料地直接：他在全员邮件中写道："我用 ChatGPT 写了这封邮件，你们也应该这样做，并且为此感到自豪……用 ChatGPT 不是作弊，那是学术界的概念，在这里不适用。"

面对 AI 取代人类的普遍焦虑，Will 用 Excel 来类比："如果你是一名做多空股票的投资者，却不会用 Excel 建财务模型，那你就已经被淘汰了。"AI 是同样的拐点，只是速度更快。工具不会消灭思考，而是给你更多思考的时间："这些工具并不否定思考的必要性，恰恰相反，它们应该给你更多时间去思考。"他还每天借助 AI 写日记，把零散思路转化成成文段落，整个过程不超过一分钟——而这在过去需要花费数小时。

他的长期愿景被他称为"博格"（Borg，致敬《星际迷航》）——一个覆盖整个组织的智能层，将每一次 Zoom 录像、每一封邮件、每一条 Slack 消息和每一份文档，持续汇入 LLM 进行实时合成与预测。目前还处于早期阶段，但 Walleye 内部的"Current"产品已经是一个可运行的概念验证。他认为，同样的逻辑适用于任何愿意认真做数据基础设施的组织。
