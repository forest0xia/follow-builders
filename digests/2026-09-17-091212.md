**Claude Blog — Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude**

Anthropic announced a major product change: Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one unified Claude. Users can bring a quick question or hand over a report due at noon, and Claude takes it from there — even after you've closed your laptop. The merger is rolling out on Pro and Max plans over the coming weeks, with more plans to follow.

Three new creation tools debut alongside the merger: Claude Docs (collaborative writing), Claude Slides (presentation creation, exportable as PowerPoint or PDF), and Claude Design (now embedded in conversations, previously a standalone tool). All three are in beta on paid plans. Enterprise admins choose when to enable them and will receive 30 days' notice before any changes.

The motivation: people used both Cowork and chat but found it frustrating to decide where a task belonged, and context didn't carry between them. Now Claude routes automatically — figuring out based on your prompt whether to give a quick answer or do deeper work, and what format suits best.

"I could have Claude pull up [my legal research database], and it would pull all the cases, read them, figure out which other cases I might need, download them, and store them in a folder for my personal review." — Andrew Keller, Senior Economist

https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude

Anthropic 宣布了一项重大产品变更：Claude Cowork 与对话正式合并为统一的 Claude。用户可以随手问一个问题，也可以把中午截止的报告交给 Claude 处理，Claude 会接手完成——即使你已经关掉电脑。此次合并正在向 Pro 和 Max 计划用户分阶段推送，更多计划将随后跟进。

合并的同时，三款新创作工具也一并亮相：Claude Docs（协作写作）、Claude Slides（演示文稿创建，可导出为 PowerPoint 或 PDF）、以及 Claude Design（现已嵌入对话，此前为独立工具）。三款工具在付费计划中均以 beta 版本提供，企业管理员自行决定启用时间，并将在任何变更前至少提前 30 天收到通知。

变革的动因：用户虽然同时使用 Cowork 和对话，但在决定某个任务该放在哪里时往往感到困扰，且两者之间的上下文无法互通。现在 Claude 会自动路由——根据你的 prompt 判断是给出快速回答还是进行更深度的工作，以及最适合的输出形式。

"我可以让 Claude 调取（我的法律研究数据库），它会找出所有相关案例，阅读它们，判断还需要哪些案例，下载下来，并存储到文件夹供我个人审阅。" —— Andrew Keller，高级经济学家
