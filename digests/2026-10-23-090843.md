**Training Data: David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence — A Superlearner for the Age of Experience**

**The Takeaway:** The co-founders of Ineffable Intelligence argue that the path to superintelligence runs through systems that discover knowledge autonomously from experience — not through scaling LLMs — and that this bet is so important it required its own dedicated lab.

David Silver, whose 2009 PhD on reinforcement learning in Go helped spark the modern deep learning era, left DeepMind to co-found Ineffable Intelligence with Lasse Espeholt. Their thesis is blunt: the world already has tens of thousands of talented researchers doubling down on LLMs. Silver asked himself, "Am I going to be the 100,001st person pursuing that approach? Is that the most effective use of my time?" The answer was no.

What Ineffable is building is what Silver calls a "superlearner" — a system that can start from zero knowledge and discover everything for itself, just as humanity collectively built all of science, language, and mathematics without a blueprint. The key distinction from current AI: it must be fully autonomous, not requiring human-in-the-loop effort to make breakthroughs, and it must be deployable into any domain — simulated, digital, or physical — and learn from experience there.

Lasse Espeholt, who pioneered early work on scaling reinforcement learning with parallelization (the architectural roots Silver says the entire field now uses), was drawn in both by the technical vision and by the rare opportunity to build something like this in Europe.

Silver believes that if this works, encountering the resulting intelligence will feel like "wonderment" — the same kind of joyful amazement Go players felt watching AlphaGo, but multiplied across every field of science and human endeavor. As he put it: "That's an incredible thing that no one has cracked yet in AI. And so to me, that is the essence of what it will take to get to superintelligence — a system that can just keep learning and discovering knowledge forever."

The company name Ineffable, Silver explained, captures two ideas: the knowledge a superintelligence might discover could be so profound it surpasses human language to express — and also, as a second meaning, "it can't possibly F up." An admirable confidence.

**Training Data：David Silver 与 Lasse Espeholt 谈 Ineffable Intelligence 与 superlearner**

**核心洞察：** Ineffable Intelligence 的联创认为，通往超级智能的道路在于能从经验中自主发现知识的系统，而非继续扩展 LLM；这个赌注足够重要，值得为它专门创立一家实验室。

David Silver 凭借 2009 年围棋强化学习博士论文点燃了现代深度学习时代，之后从 DeepMind 出走，与 Lasse Espeholt 共同创立 Ineffable Intelligence。他们的论点直截了当：全球已有数以万计的优秀研究者在押注 LLM。Silver 问自己："我要成为追求同一路径的第 100,001 个人吗？这是对我时间和精力的最佳利用吗？"答案是否定的。

Ineffable 正在构建的是 Silver 所称的"superlearner"——一个从零知识出发、能够自主发现一切的系统，就像人类集体在没有蓝图的情况下构建了全部的科学、语言和数学体系。与当前 AI 的关键区别：它必须完全自主，无需人工介入即可实现突破；并且必须能够被部署到任何领域——模拟的、数字的或物理的——并在其中从经验中学习。

Lasse Espeholt 是并行化强化学习早期扩展工作的先驱——Silver 说，今天整个领域使用的架构根源都可追溯到 Lasse 当年的工作。他加入 Ineffable，既被技术愿景吸引，也被在欧洲打造这样一家公司的稀缺机会所打动。

Silver 相信，如果成功，与这种智能的首次接触将带来"惊叹感"——就像围棋高手目睹 AlphaGo 对弈时那种喜悦与震撼，但会蔓延到科学和人类事业的每一个领域。正如他所说："没有任何人在 AI 中破解了这件事。而对我来说，这正是通往超级智能所需的核心——一个能够永无止境地学习和发现知识的系统。"

公司名"Ineffable"蕴含两层含义：超级智能发现的知识可能如此深刻，以至于超越人类语言所能表达的范围；另一层"秘密含义"则是，这条路"根本不可能搞砸"。一种令人钦佩的自信。
