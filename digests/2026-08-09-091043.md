**No Priors — "Chasing Trillion-Dollar Companies, Founder Ambition, Token Budgets, and Regulatory Capture"**

**The Takeaway:** We just lived through a historically anomalous five-year window where several companies went from zero to a trillion dollars in market cap — and that window is probably closing, not reopening.

No Priors hosts Sarah Guo (Conviction VC) and Elad Gil sit down for a candid two-person conversation spanning venture math, AI safety, founder ambition, and the economic future of California.

On trillion-dollar companies, Elad is deliberate and contrarian: the AI wave already produced its consolidators (Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceX). Getting another company to $50–100B in revenue — the floor for trillion-dollar market cap — is structurally different from just building a big TAM story. Investors, he argues, are conflating "this is a huge market" with "this company can get there fast enough." Most can't. Some will reach $20B or $100B, which is still massive — but multi-trillion outcomes in a 3-5 year window are rare by definition.

Sarah pushes back with a failure-of-imagination framing: most investors still think linearly about market size, using per-seat or per-doctor TAMs when they should be asking what outcome-based pricing and AI-delivered services could actually look like at scale. Coding, both agree, proved that consumption and value can expand 100x from what anyone originally modeled.

On token budgets and return on invested compute, Elad introduces a concept worth holding onto: "return on invested tokens" — the idea that compute is now the scarce resource (not the researcher), and whoever decides who gets token budget is making the same call that engineering managers used to make about where to deploy engineers: product vs. internal tools.

On regulatory capture and California's billionaire tax, both hosts are concerned. Sarah flags that regulators haven't thought through execution or compliance. Elad sees the bill as broadly enough written that future legislatures can lower the threshold, and notes exit taxes are being discussed for 2028. He expects meaningful founder migration, but thinks it will take time — the people who wrote the bill may actually *want* the ecosystem to leave.

**No Priors —《追逐万亿美元公司、创始人雄心、token 预算与监管俘获》**

**核心洞察：** 我们刚刚经历了一个历史性异常的五年窗口期——多家公司从零成长为万亿级市值——而这个窗口很可能正在关闭，而非重启。

No Priors 主播 Sarah Guo（Conviction VC）和 Elad Gil 进行了一场坦诚的双人对话，话题横跨风险投资逻辑、AI 安全、创始人雄心以及加州的经济未来。

关于万亿美元公司，Elad 的判断清醒且逆主流：AI 浪潮已经产生了它的整合者（Anthropic、OpenAI、SpaceX）。要让另一家公司实现 500-1000 亿美元营收——万亿美元市值的底线——在结构上与讲一个大 TAM 故事是完全不同的事情。他认为，投资人正在混淆"市场很大"和"这家公司能足够快地到达那里"。大多数公司做不到。有些公司会达到 200 亿或 1000 亿，这已经很惊人——但在 3-5 年内出现万亿级结果，本质上就是罕见的。

Sarah 则从"想象力失败"的角度提出反驳：大多数投资人依然线性地看待市场规模，用每个席位或每位医生的 TAM 来衡量，而不是思考基于结果的定价和 AI 交付的服务在规模化后究竟能做到什么。两人都同意，编程领域已经证明：消费和价值可以从最初的模型预测扩大 100 倍。

关于 token 预算和计算资源的回报，Elad 提出了一个值得深思的概念："投入 token 的回报"——现在真正稀缺的资源是算力而非研究员，决定谁能获得 token 预算的人，正在做着工程经理过去的决定：把工程师投入到产品还是内部工具。

关于监管俘获和加州亿万富翁税，两位主播都表达了担忧。Sarah 指出监管者并未充分考虑执行和合规问题。Elad 认为该法案措辞足够宽泛，未来立法机构可以进一步降低门槛，并提到 2028 年可能会讨论离境税。他预计创始人的迁移将是真实且持续的，但需要时间——而撰写该法案的人，也许本就希望这个生态圈离开。
