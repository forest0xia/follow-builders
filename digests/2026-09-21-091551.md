**No Priors — "Coinbase's Everything Exchange: Agentic Finance, Stablecoins, and Tokenization with CEO Brian Armstrong"**

The Takeaway: Crypto rails are positioning themselves as the essential financial infrastructure for the AI agent economy — and Brian Armstrong is already building for a world where agents outnumber humans.

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong argues we are at an inflection point where every asset class is migrating on-chain: stocks, commodities, derivatives, and prediction markets. The more provocative case he makes is about the agentic economy. An estimated 76% of agent ecommerce transactions are under 30 cents — too small for traditional card rails, which carry a 30-cent minimum fee. That is not a niche problem; Armstrong believes agents will soon outnumber humans, making this the largest payment infrastructure challenge in history.

"We don't want the AIs to be unbanked." Coinbase has already built tools that let any AI agent open its own financial account with a single prompt — no KYC, no government ID, self-custodial wallet on crypto rails. The protocol underpinning this, X402 (incubated at Coinbase, now in the Linux Foundation), has attracted Google, Cloudflare, and AWS as collaborators.

Inside Coinbase, Armstrong has been pursuing what he calls "recursive self-improvement." Every time an agent modifies a service, it ingests a "brain" — a structured knowledge base of incidents, financial controls, AB tests, and PR history for that repository. When a human reviewer catches something the agent missed, that correction feeds back into the brain. Over time, one-shot PR acceptance rates tick up automatically.

On longevity: Armstrong's other company, New Limit, is focused on epigenetic reprogramming — restoring the biological function cells had when the body was younger. It is preparing its first Phase 1 clinical trial next year targeting alcohol liver disease, with a broader ambition to attack aging as the root cause of most major diseases, rather than treating symptoms one by one.
