**Madhu Guru**, Senior Director of AI at Meta (previously led Gemini, Veo, and Nano at Google), published part 8 of his "How to build great evals" series, focusing on what he calls the *discriminatory property* of evaluations. His argument: an eval that gives similar scores to great and mediocre AI systems is useless — like giving a PhD a fifth-grade math test. The sweet spot is evals that are "realistic + difficult + sensitive to differences in capability." He challenged readers to think about what to do when even good evals eventually saturate as models and harnesses improve.
