### No Priors: Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg

**The Takeaway:** Drug discovery is stuck in an empirical loop that AI can break — and Isomorphic Labs is betting that a machine-learning platform, not just better tools, is what finally compounds progress in medicine the way software compounds progress in tech.

Max Jaderberg is president and co-founder of Isomorphic Labs, the Alphabet-backed company founded in 2021 with the stated mission of solving all disease. He came up through DeepMind, working on AlphaGo and AlphaStar, and watched AlphaFold become the proof of concept that convinced him deep learning could generalize in biology. What he's building now is the logical extension of that bet.

The pharmaceutical industry has a strange paradox: the number of approved drugs per year has stayed flat even as scientific knowledge exploded, drug modality options multiplied, and costs ballooned from under $200 million per drug to $1.5 billion, with timelines stretching from 8–9 years to 15. Jaderberg's diagnosis is that the industry still runs on trial and error. You have a hypothesis about a disease, you design a molecule, you make it in the lab, you test it in the real world — and you repeat this loop until you're out of money or luck. "The cell is a big complex chaotic system. Super high dimensional. And the only tools we've got available to us is basically trial and error screening processes." Each drug program is treated as a bespoke frontier science project. Very little of what you learn carries forward.

That's exactly what Isomorphic wants to change. The vision isn't to automate one step in the process — it's to build an AI platform that compounds knowledge across every drug program, so each new medicine makes the next one faster and better. The analogy to foundation models is intentional: just as scale and generalization transformed text and images, Jaderberg believes the same dynamics can hit biology if the underlying model is absorbing enough signal from enough programs.

One counterintuitive hiring insight: because "AI for drug discovery" as a discipline barely exists, Isomorphic doesn't go looking for it. They hire the best core AI researchers — people without biology backgrounds — and pair them with top biologists and chemists who've never touched ML. The cross-disciplinary understanding that emerges internally is the differentiated asset. As Jaderberg put it: "Internally we get these cross-disciplinary experts emerging, which has been really exciting to see."

On timelines, he won't give a hard date for a first clinical trial, but he's clear about what five years should look like: "I'd be really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time and the predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in a massive way." That's not a hedge. It's a standard he's setting for himself.

**No Priors 播客：Isomorphic Labs 总裁 Max Jaderberg 谈 AI 驱动的药物研发**

**核心要点：** 药物研发长期被困在经验主义的循环中，而 AI 有望打破这一局面。Isomorphic Labs 正在押注：一个机器学习平台（而不仅仅是更好的工具）才能让医学领域实现像软件一样的复利式进步。

Max Jaderberg 是 Isomorphic Labs 的总裁和联合创始人，这家由 Alphabet 支持的公司成立于 2021 年，使命是解决所有疾病。他在 DeepMind 成长起来，参与了 AlphaGo 和 AlphaStar 的研发，也亲眼看到 AlphaFold 成为深度学习能在生物学领域泛化的关键证据，从而让他坚定了现在正在做的事。

制药行业存在一个奇怪的悖论：即使科学知识爆炸式增长、药物研发模式不断涌现，每年获批的新药数量依然停滞不前，同时单个药物的开发成本从不到 2 亿美元飙升至 15 亿美元，周期也从 8-9 年延长到 15 年。Jaderberg 的诊断是：这个行业仍在依赖试错。你对疾病提出假设，设计分子，在实验室合成，放到真实世界测试，然后循环往复直到资金耗尽。"细胞是一个极其复杂的混沌系统，维度超高。而我们拥有的唯一工具，基本上就是试错筛选流程。" 每个药物项目都被当作独立的前沿科学工程，几乎没有什么经验可以传递给下一个项目。

这正是 Isomorphic 想要改变的。他们的愿景不是自动化流程中的某一步，而是构建一个能在每个药物项目之间复利积累知识的 AI 平台，让每一款新药都能让下一款更快、更好。这个类比是有意为之的：就像规模化和泛化能力改变了文字和图像处理，Jaderberg 相信，只要底层模型从足够多的项目中吸收足够多的信号，同样的动力学规律也能在生物学中发生。

一个反直觉的招聘洞察："AI + 药物研发"这个交叉领域几乎不存在现成的专家，所以 Isomorphic 根本不去寻找。他们聘用最优秀的核心 AI 研究员（往往没有生物学背景），再搭配从未接触过 ML 的顶级生物学家和化学家。内部涌现出的跨学科理解才是他们真正的差异化资产。正如 Jaderberg 所说："内部逐渐涌现出跨学科的专家科学家，这让人非常振奋。"

在时间线方面，他不愿给出首个临床试验的具体日期，但对五年后的期望非常清晰："如果五年后，对疾病生物学的预测性理解没有发生重大跨越，我会非常失望。" 这不是在逃避，而是他为自己设定的标准。
