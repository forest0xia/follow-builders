**No Priors: Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference**
*with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon*

**The Takeaway:** Diffusion models are not just for image generation — they are built to outcompete autoregressive LLMs at inference time, and Stefano Ermon has been working toward this moment since 2019.

Ermon is one of the academic fathers of diffusion models. As a Stanford professor starting in 2014, he and his PhD student Yang Song pioneered training neural networks to denoise data — a framework that became the backbone of Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, and much of today's generative AI. In 2024, his lab showed that diffusion-based language models could match GPT-2-scale autoregressive models in text quality while generating tokens 10x faster. That result led him to found Inception, a ~50-person company now commercializing diffusion LLMs under the Mercury product line.

His core argument: autoregressive models are fundamentally parallelism-limited at inference time. "The workload is extremely memory bound. You're spending most of your time moving around weights across the memory hierarchy, and you're doing very little arithmetic." Diffusion models generate many tokens in parallel, mapping far better to GPUs — the same architectural shift, he argues, that separated Transformers from RNNs at training time. "The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

Mercury models currently match the speed-optimized tiers from frontier labs (Haiku, Flash, Mini, Nano) in quality while running significantly faster. Voice AI startup OpenCall switched from custom Cerebras hardware to Mercury and now hits the same latency on standard NVIDIA GPUs at lower cost and higher availability.

Beyond speed, Ermon sees latent advantages still being mapped: diffusion models may be more data-efficient (each training sample generates many noisy views, a form of built-in augmentation) and more controllable (generation can be steered coarse-to-fine with a reward signal, rather than only evaluated at the end). Whether diffusion LLMs will eventually surpass autoregressive ones in raw capability is unknown. That is precisely why Ermon finds it exciting.

**No Priors：为什么 Diffusion 将赢得 AI 推理之战**
*嘉宾：Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO Stefano Ermon*

**核心观点：** Diffusion 模型不只适用于图像生成——它们在推理效率上具备超越自回归 LLM 的根本性优势，而 Ermon 从 2019 年起就一直在为这一时刻做准备。

Ermon 是 diffusion 模型的学术奠基人之一。2014 年起他在斯坦福担任教授，与博士生 Yang Song 共同开创了训练神经网络去噪的框架——这一框架成为 Stable Diffusion、Midjourney 以及当今大多数生成式 AI 的技术基础。2024 年，他的实验室发表成果证明：基于 diffusion 的语言模型可在文本质量上媲美 GPT-2 规模的自回归模型，同时生成速度快 10 倍。这一结果促使他创立了 Inception，一家约 50 人的公司，正在通过 Mercury 系列产品将 diffusion LLM 商业化。

他的核心论点：自回归模型在推理阶段存在根本性的并行瓶颈。"其工作负载极度受限于内存带宽，大量时间消耗在跨内存层次移动权重上，真正进行算术运算的时间极少。" Diffusion 模型并行生成多个 token，能更好地映射到 GPU 架构——他认为这与训练阶段 Transformer 超越 RNN 的逻辑如出一辙。"更具并行性的方案最终会赢，这是苦涩的教训。"

Mercury 模型目前在质量上匹配前沿实验室的速度优化版本（Haiku、Flash、Mini、Nano），同时速度更快。语音 AI 初创公司 OpenCall 从 Cerebras 定制芯片切换到 Mercury 后，在标准 NVIDIA GPU 上获得了相同的延迟表现，且成本更低、可用性更高。

在速度之外，Ermon 还看到一些尚待验证的潜在优势：diffusion 模型可能更具数据效率（每个训练样本通过多种噪声视图形成内置数据增强），也更易于控制（可从粗到细用奖励信号引导生成，而非只能对最终输出评分）。diffusion LLM 是否最终会在原始能力上超越自回归模型，目前尚无定论。而这正是 Ermon 觉得最令人兴奋之处。
