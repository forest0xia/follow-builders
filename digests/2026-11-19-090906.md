### AI & I by Every — "Notion's Founder on the One Thing Your AI Is Missing"

**The Takeaway:** The missing ingredient in most AI tools isn't more intelligence — it's your data, your context, and your knowledge. Notion CEO Ivan Zhao is repositioning the company as the durable "context layer" for businesses in an agent-first world.

**The Takeaway：** 大多数 AI 工具缺失的关键要素不是更强的智能——而是你的数据、你的上下文和你的知识。Notion CEO Ivan Zhao 正在将公司重新定位为 agent 优先时代企业持久的"上下文层"。

Ivan Zhao founded Notion with a 1960s vision: computing should be as universal as reading and writing. Vibe coding, he argues, is finally making that vision real — but it comes with a catch. Building the demo is the easy part; "good software is a set of strong opinions," and most people don't know what they actually want until they've built something wrong. He splits software into three buckets: things worth customizing yourself (personal tools), things you want expertly crafted by someone else (calendar, well-designed apps), and enterprise systems too costly to self-host.

Ivan Zhao 创立 Notion 源于一个 1960 年代的愿景：计算应该像读写一样普及。他认为，Vibe coding 终于让这一愿景成真了——但随之而来一个陷阱。做 demo 只是开始；"好的软件是一套强烈的主见"，大多数人在做错了一遍之前根本不知道自己真正想要什么。他把软件分为三类：值得自己定制的（个人工具）、应该由专家精心打磨的（日历、设计精良的应用），以及自托管成本太高的企业级系统。

Where Notion is placing its bet: not on the UI layer (which agents can generate) or the procedural logic (which LLMs can write), but on the data. "What's really important is the data. How do you hold your data? How do you hold your context, your semantics?" Zhao sees Notion as the keeper of business context — opening it up to whatever agents the world sends its way. His near-term goal: a 50-person company should be able to onboard through an agent and have all its knowledge and context assembled out of the box within two quarters.

Notion 押注的不是 UI 层（agent 可以生成）也不是过程逻辑（LLM 可以编写），而是数据本身。"真正重要的是数据。你如何持有你的数据？如何保存你的上下文、你的语义？"Zhao 将 Notion 定位为企业上下文的守护者——向世界上任何 agent 开放访问。他的近期目标：一家 50 人的公司应该能够通过 agent 完成入驻，并在两个季度内开箱即用地将所有知识和上下文整合到位。

His most memorable line on the second-order effects of AI: "I've noticed myself like to chat with this thing more than I like to read... Sometimes it feels like I'm on TikTok. On TikTok, after 10 minutes, you get grossed out. I got grossed out. It feels like that a little bit." His call to builders: don't just hand people the clay — figure out what the "Legos of vibe coding" look like. Primitives with structure baked in, strong enough to build tall, opinionated enough to prevent the whole thing from collapsing.

他关于 AI 二阶效应最令人印象深刻的一句话："我发现自己喜欢和这个东西聊天多过喜欢阅读……有时候感觉就像刷 TikTok。刷 TikTok 刷十分钟会觉得恶心，我也有这种感觉。"他对 builder 们的呼吁：不要只给人们提供黏土——想清楚"vibe coding 的乐高"是什么样的。那应该是内置结构的基础模块，坚固到可以搭高，主见强到不会整个垮塌。
