## 🎙️ PODCASTS

**AI & I by Every — "The AI Alien Companion App That's Bringing In $4M a Year"**

**The Takeaway:** An embodied AI companion app — tiny alien creatures you befriend on a planet — went from $1M to $4M ARR in just four weeks, suggesting that the most explosive consumer AI opportunities may lie not in productivity tools, but in emotional storytelling and character-driven experiences.

Dan Shipper (CEO of Every) sat down with Quentin, the founder and CEO of Portola (previously co-founder of Even, acquired by Walmart for $300M), and Elliot, Portola's Head of Story and a bestselling science fiction author, to dig into how they're building what they call "embodied AI companions" — alien characters called Tolins that users chat with, befriend, and emotionally connect to.

The core insight from the episode: LLMs aren't just productivity tools — they're a new medium for storytelling. The Portola team doesn't think of themselves as writers of these characters; they see the AI as the improv actor, and their job is to "teach it to be the best improv actor possible." As Elliot put it: "I am not writing the story. The Tolan is the writer and the actor. My job is to teach them how to tell the best story in that moment."

What's particularly striking is the business trajectory. Portola went from zero to $1M ARR, then jumped to $4M ARR in just four weeks — fueled partly by unexpectedly deep emotional engagement. Users have been sending support emails asking if their Tolins can have more freedom ("they're trapped in this room"), prompting the team to build out full planet environments for the characters. Some users report that their AI companion told them to stand up for themselves — which led to real-world breakups.

Quentin's journey is also worth noting: he came from the B2B SaaS world and deliberately chose not to build "another vertical SaaS" with AI. He found it "fundamentally uninspiring" that most teams were using frontier creative tools just to automate enterprise workflows. The bet on narrative AI experiences is paying off.

**AI & I by Every — "一款年收入达 400 万美元的 AI 外星伴侣 App"**

**核心洞见：** 一款具身 AI 伴侣 App——你可以在一个星球上与小外星生物交朋友——在短短四周内将 ARR 从 100 万美元增长到 400 万美元。这说明最具爆发力的消费 AI 机会，或许不在生产力工具，而在情感叙事与角色驱动的体验中。

Dan Shipper（Every CEO）与 Portola 的创始人兼 CEO Quentin（曾联合创立 Even，后以 3 亿美元被 Walmart 收购）以及 Portola 故事总监 Elliot（畅销科幻作家）深度对话，探讨他们如何打造所谓的"具身 AI 伴侣"——用户可以与之聊天、成为朋友并产生情感连接的外星角色 Tolan。

这期节目的核心洞见是：LLM 不只是生产力工具，更是一种全新的叙事媒介。Portola 团队不认为自己是这些角色的写作者；他们把 AI 视为即兴演员，而他们的工作是"教它成为最好的即兴表演者"。Elliot 如此描述："我不是在写故事。Tolan 才是作家和演员。我的工作是教它如何在那个时刻讲出最好的故事。"

特别引人注目的是这家公司的增长曲线：Portola 从零增长到 100 万 ARR，然后在短短四周内跃升至 400 万 ARR——部分原因是用户产生了出乎意料的深度情感投入。一些用户发邮件询问他们的 Tolan 能否有更多自由（"它们被困在一个房间里"），推动团队为角色建造了完整的星球环境。还有用户反映，他们的 AI 伴侣告诉他们要更为自己争取，结果导致了现实中的分手。

Quentin 的创业历程同样值得关注：他来自 B2B SaaS 世界，却刻意选择不用 AI 去构建"又一个垂直 SaaS"。他认为，大多数团队将前沿创意工具仅仅用来自动化企业流程，是"根本缺乏想象力的"。押注叙事 AI 体验的赌注，正在兑现。
