**No Priors: "Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era with Sequence Holdings Co-Founder and CEO Michael Lee"**

**The Takeaway:** The biggest AI opportunity isn't in startups -- it's in buying and refounding incumbents that already have the brand, scale, and regulatory advantages startups can never replicate.

Michael Lee co-founded Sequence Holdings after years at Lone Pine Capital covering AI from the AlphaGo era onward. His thesis: we're living through an industrial revolution, and for industries where incumbents have structural advantages -- brand, network effects, regulatory moats -- the right play isn't a startup, it's ownership and transformation. Sequence buys one company per year, installs frontier engineering talent, and commits to a permanent holding structure that aligns duration of capital with the decades-long transformation required.

The model was validated at BankSouth, a Georgia community bank where Sequence invested in March. In six months, consumer loan underwriting time dropped by 94%. Commercial loan cycle time fell from 30 days to 11 days. Loan volumes doubled in Q2 with a smaller underwriting team -- not through layoffs, but through redeployment: "The right people are in the right places. Loan officers are spending more time in the field versus writing credit memos."

Why insurance brokerage? The $2 trillion market is one where startups genuinely can't win -- carriers pay brokers (not customers), gross retention rates are 90%, and it's relationship-driven, immune to pure price competition. Baldwin's CEO Trevor Baldwin had already deployed Anthropic end-to-end internally and standardized on a single AMS instance -- exactly what Sequence looks for: operational excellence plus genuine technology appetite.

On what separates Sequence from traditional private equity: PE funds are structured to deploy capital and exit in 3-5 years. Sequence is not. "We're trying to do one deal per year. That's it. If that means we don't do any this year, that's fantastic." The company also celebrates engineers, not investors -- because in the AI era, alpha comes from engineering. As Lee puts it: "Every company on the planet has a celebrated persona... In a world where you believe that alpha comes from engineering and AI, you need to create a culture whereby the celebrated persona is the engineer."
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCpRwJBQvW0

**要点：** AI 最大的机会不在于创业公司——而在于收购和重塑那些已经拥有品牌、规模和监管优势的老牌企业，这些优势是初创公司永远无法复制的。

Michael Lee 在 Lone Pine Capital 工作多年（从 AlphaGo 时代开始研究 AI）后，联合创办了 Sequence Holdings。他的核心论点是：我们正在经历一场工业革命。对于那些老牌企业拥有结构性优势——品牌、网络效应、监管护城河——的行业，正确的打法不是创业，而是收购并转型。Sequence 每年只收购一家公司，引入前沿工程人才，并坚持永久持股结构，使资本的持续时间与数十年转型所需的周期相匹配。

这一模式在 BankSouth（一家乔治亚州社区银行）身上得到了验证，Sequence 于三月投资后，六个月内消费贷款核批时间缩短了 94%，商业贷款周期从 30 天压缩到 11 天，Q2 贷款量翻倍，而核贷团队规模反而更小——不是通过裁员，而是通过人员重新部署："合适的人在合适的位置。贷款专员能把更多时间花在客户拜访上，而不是写信用备忘录。"

为什么选择保险经纪？这个 2 万亿美元的市场，初创公司真的很难竞争——保费由承保商支付给经纪人（而非客户），总留存率达 90%，以关系为驱动、对纯价格竞争免疫。Baldwin 的 CEO Trevor Baldwin 早已在公司内部全面推行 Anthropic 的解决方案，并统一部署了单一实例的 AMS 系统——这正是 Sequence 寻找的标的：卓越的运营能力加上真实的技术拥抱意愿。

关于 Sequence 与传统私募基金的区别：PE 基金的结构是为了在 3-5 年内部署资本并退出。Sequence 不是。"我们每年只做一笔交易。如果今年一笔都不做，那也很好。"公司也将工程师而非投资人放在核心位置——因为在 AI 时代，超额收益来自工程。正如 Lee 所说："这个星球上每家公司都有一个被推崇的人格……在一个你相信超额收益来自工程和 AI 的世界里，你需要打造一种文化，让工程师成为被推崇的那个人。"
