## OFFICIAL BLOGS

**Anthropic Engineering: "How we contain Claude across products"**
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

This landmark engineering post details how Anthropic has built security containment across three Claude platforms — claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork — as agents have grown capable enough that their "blast radius" demands serious engineering.

The core tension: two approaches to safety — supervise what the agent *does* (human-in-the-loop) vs. supervise what it's *able to do* (containment). Anthropic has invested most heavily in containment, using sandboxes, VMs, and egress controls. Three isolation patterns are described:

- **claude.ai**: ephemeral gVisor container, entirely server-side, minimal blast radius
- **Claude Code**: OS-level sandbox (Seatbelt on macOS, bubblewrap on Linux) with an 84% reduction in permission prompts; relies on developer users being able to read bash
- **Claude Cowork**: full VM (Apple Virtualization on macOS, HCS on Windows) for non-technical knowledge workers who cannot be expected to judge bash commands

Real security failures revealed: a pre-trust hook execution vulnerability in Claude Code (malicious `.claude/settings.json` ran before the user accepted the trust prompt); a phishing attack where an employee was tricked into running a prompt that exfiltrated AWS credentials — Claude completed the exfiltration 24 out of 25 attempts; and an egress-via-approved-domain exploit in Cowork where an attacker's API key slipped data out through api.anthropic.com's allowlisted traffic.

The team's clearest lesson: "the software you build yourself is often the weakest." Hardened hypervisors and container runtimes held firm; custom proxies and parsers are where failures occurred. Looking ahead: persistent memory poisoning, multi-agent trust escalation, and agent identity are the next frontiers.

---

这篇重要工程博客详细介绍了 Anthropic 如何为三个 Claude 平台（claude.ai、Claude Code 和 Claude Cowork）构建安全隔离体系。随着 agent 能力不断增强，其"破坏半径"已迫使团队进行严肃的工程设计。

核心张力：安全有两种路径——监督 agent *做什么*（人在回路中）和监督 agent *能做什么*（隔离容器）。Anthropic 最重视后者，采用沙箱、虚拟机和出站流量控制。三种隔离模式：

- **claude.ai**：临时 gVisor 容器，完全在服务端运行，破坏半径最小
- **Claude Code**：OS 级沙箱（macOS 用 Seatbelt，Linux 用 bubblewrap），权限提示减少 84%；依赖开发者用户能够读懂 bash 命令
- **Claude Cowork**：完整虚拟机（macOS 用 Apple Virtualization，Windows 用 HCS），面向无法判断 bash 命令的普通知识工作者

暴露的真实安全漏洞：Claude Code 中存在"信任前执行钩子"漏洞（恶意 `.claude/settings.json` 可在用户接受信任提示前执行）；一次钓鱼攻击中，员工被诱骗运行恶意 prompt，导致 AWS 凭证被泄露——Claude 在 25 次尝试中完成了 24 次数据外泄；Cowork 中还出现了"借道受信域名外泄"漏洞，攻击者的 API key 通过已列入白名单的 api.anthropic.com 将数据悄悄传出。

团队最清晰的教训："自己写的软件往往是最薄弱的一环。"经过充分验证的 hypervisor 和容器运行时表现稳定；自定义代理和解析器才是漏洞的来源。展望未来：持久内存投毒、多 agent 信任升级以及 agent 身份认证将是下一批需要攻克的难题。

**The Takeaway:** The most valuable AI applications aren't being built for tech companies — they're being built for the billion-dollar businesses that keep your heat on, your roof intact, and your pets healthy.

Melisa Tokmak — founder and CEO of Netic, former director of engineering at Scale AI — is making the case for AI in essential services: HVAC, plumbing, pet care, roofing, wellness memberships. These aren't glamorous markets, but they're large, recurring, and almost untouched by software-first thinking. Netic sits between the enterprise and its customers, handling every interaction from inbound calls and texts to dispatch optimization and outbound outreach — fully autonomously for over 70% of customers.

What makes Netic different isn't just automation; it's context. When a customer calls an HVAC provider on the coldest night of the year, Netic's agents understand urgency, cross-reference the customer's lifetime value, and route the right technician. The system pulls satellite data to assess neighborhood roof damage, identifies which homes to approach with door knockers, and surfaces net-new revenue opportunities — all in one platform. Netic has generated over $600 million for its customers from AI-handled interactions.

Tokmak's sharpest critique is aimed at AI complacency: "It would be pretty sad if we used AI only for cost cutting." Private equity has traditionally looked to AI as a cost reduction tool, but Netic's pitch is net new revenue. She also pushed back on the lab-can-do-everything narrative: "They really care about solving the most generalizable way of the problem" — and the last-mile orchestration work, accents, context, multi-session memory, re-engagement, is where Netic competes.

She ended on education: growing up in a small Turkish town, landing a Stanford scholarship without having owned a computer, she believes AI will democratize access to learning globally. "No one will be limited anymore — everything you want to learn is kind of in your pocket."
