**Aaron Levie (CEO of Box)**

Box CEO Aaron Levie published a detailed analysis of the accelerating divergence in AI productivity. **Unsupervised Learning — Ep 96: Nebius CTO on Racing the Hyperscalers Before AWS Wakes Up**

**The Takeaway:** The real bottleneck in building an AI cloud isn't GPUs, power, or talent in isolation — it's your organizational ability to execute across all of them simultaneously.

Nebius CTO Neela (the company formerly known as the international arm of Yandex) makes a case that "AI cloud" is a category worth fighting for — and that the window to get ahead of AWS and Google Cloud is now, while chip shortages constrain the hyperscalers' ability to dedicate capacity to this market. Nebius started with a hardware-experienced core from Yandex but became a software-first company: their hardware team at peak was 40–50 people; software engineering was already 200.

Their view on inference is notably unsentimental: "As an industry, we didn't optimize shit." Running open-weight models naively gives underwhelming results. The real work lies in kernel optimizations, model pruning, selective compression — particularly for the 95% of enterprise customers who are running fine-tuned, domain-specific variants, not vanilla open-source models. For these customers, what matters isn't maximum intelligence across all tasks; it's predictable performance on a narrow domain.

On AI coding and agentic tools: Neela is deliberately cautious about fully autonomous agents touching core services, requiring that anything created by an autonomous agent be explicitly marked as such, with the engineer still accountable. "I've seen how this sausage is made, and I'll be cautious."

His most interesting forward bet: the value creation in the next wave of AI won't come from bigger models, but from optimizing the entire outcome loop around agents — the harnesses, tools, and orchestration that determine how reliably a long-running agent reaches its goal.

https://www.youtube.com/@RedpointAI

**要点：** 构建 AI 云的真正瓶颈不是单独的 GPU、电力或人才短缺——而是你的组织在这一切上同步执行的能力。

Nebius CTO Neela（该公司前身为 Yandex 国际业务）主张"AI 云"是一个值得全力争夺的赛道——而在 AWS 和 Google Cloud 真正发力之前，窗口期就是现在，芯片短缺正限制着超大规模云厂商向这一市场分配资源的能力。Nebius 以 Yandex 硬件经验团队为核心起步，但逐渐演变为软件优先的公司：高峰期硬件团队只有 40–50 人，软件工程团队已达 200 人。

他对 inference 的看法毫不留情："作为一个行业，我们根本没有做好优化。"直接运行原始开源模型效果平平。真正的工作在于 kernel 优化、模型剪枝、选择性压缩——尤其是针对 95% 运行微调后领域专用模型的企业客户，他们要的不是在所有任务上的最大智能，而是在特定领域的可预测性能。

在 AI 编程和 agent 工具方面，Neela 对完全自主 agent 接触核心服务持审慎态度，要求自主 agent 生成的任何内容必须明确标注，工程师依然承担责任。"我见过这个'香肠'是怎么做出来的，我会保持谨慎。"

他最值得关注的前瞻性判断：下一波 AI 价值创造不会来自更大的模型，而是来自对整个 agent 结果循环的优化——那些决定长时运行 agent 能否可靠达成目标的 harness、工具和编排层。
