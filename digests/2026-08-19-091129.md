**Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products**

Anthropic Engineering published a deep technical post on how it manages Claude's blast radius as agents gain more access. The core insight: a year ago, granting Claude enough access to take down an internal Anthropic service would have been unthinkable — today it's routine. The post outlines two containment strategies. First, human-in-the-loop supervision: Claude Code originally asked users for permission at each turn, but telemetry showed users approved ~93% of prompts, growing less diligent over time. This led to the new Claude Code auto mode, which automates safer approvals to reduce approval fatigue while maintaining genuine safety oversight. Second, environment-level containment: bounding what actions an agent can take at the infrastructure level. The post also references Claude Mythos Preview, a model whose blast radius was deemed too high to ship in April 2026 — but signals that models of similar capability will become appropriate to release as defenders harden critical systems.

**Anthropic Engineering：我们如何在各产品中控制 Claude 的影响范围**

Anthropic Engineering 发布了一篇深度技术文章，讲述随着 agent 获得更多访问权限，公司如何管理 Claude 的"爆炸半径"。核心洞察：一年前，赋予 Claude 足以影响 Anthropic 内部服务的访问权限是不可想象的——今天已是常规操作。文章概述了两种控制策略。其一是人在回路监督：Claude Code 最初要求用户逐步审批每个操作，但遥测数据显示用户批准了约 93% 的提示请求，且随着时间推移愈发不认真审查。这促使团队推出 Claude Code 自动模式，自动处理更安全的审批，减少审批疲劳，同时保持真正的安全监督。其二是环境级隔离：在基础设施层面限制 agent 可执行的操作范围。文章还提到了 Claude Mythos Preview——一个在 2026 年 4 月因爆炸半径过大而未能发布的模型，但同时暗示，随着防守方加固关键系统，具有类似能力的模型将逐步具备发布条件。

**Training Data: Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed — Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xH7U7w9Qzlo

**The Takeaway:** Rich Sutton — inventor of reinforcement learning, author of *The Bitter Lesson*, and Turing Award winner — argues that today's LLMs have a fundamental flaw: they don't actually learn. They are trained, frozen, and deployed. What the field calls "continual learning" is, to Sutton, simply *learning* — because all real intelligence learns continuously from ongoing experience. The prevailing paradigm, he says, is the weird one.

Rich Sutton survived cancer in 2003 while in an AI winter, joined the University of Alberta, and spent decades building the theoretical foundations of reinforcement learning before the world caught up. His former student and co-founder Khurram Javed joins this episode — a collaboration that began organically over a shared research project and evolved into Oak Lab, their new company.

The episode covers *The Bitter Lesson* — Sutton's 2019 essay arguing that methods leveraging computation always beat human-knowledge-engineered methods in the long run — and its continued relevance in the LLM era. Sutton does not view LLMs as the end of the road. His concern is that LLMs are trained on a fixed dataset and then fixed in place. They don't adapt from ongoing interaction. The failure mode he's focused on is *plasticity loss* — the tendency of neural networks to become less capable of updating weights over time, even if they're nominally in training. Javed's PhD work at Alberta directly targeted this: how do you keep a network capable of learning indefinitely, without it forgetting what it already knows?

Oak Lab was founded to build AI systems that never stop learning — agents that can interact with their environment continuously, update from experience, and maintain the kind of fluid, ongoing intelligence that biological learning represents. Sutton's view is that the field will eventually be forced in this direction, as the limitations of static training become clearer. His characteristic stance: "I'm not weird. The field is weird."

**Training Data：Rich Sutton 与 Khurram Javed——为什么 AI 模型停止了学习，以及如何让它重新开始**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xH7U7w9Qzlo

**核心洞察：** 强化学习发明者、《苦涩的教训》作者、图灵奖得主 Rich Sutton 认为，当今 LLM 存在根本性缺陷：它们根本没有在学习。它们被训练、冻结、部署。所谓"持续学习"，在 Sutton 看来只是"学习"本身——因为所有真正的智能都在从持续的经历中不断学习。他说，主流范式才是那个奇怪的存在。

Rich Sutton 在 2003 年 AI 寒冬期间从癌症中活了下来，加入阿尔伯塔大学，花了数十年时间奠定强化学习的理论基础，最终等来了世界的追上。他的前学生兼联合创始人 Khurram Javed 出席了本期节目——两人的合作始于一个共同的研究项目，最终演变成他们共同创立的 Oak Lab。

本期涵盖《苦涩的教训》——Sutton 2019 年提出"利用计算力的方法长期来看总是优于依赖人类知识的工程化方法"的论断——以及这一论断在 LLM 时代的持续意义。Sutton 并不认为 LLM 是终点。他的担忧在于：LLM 在固定数据集上训练完成后便固定不变，无法从持续的交互中适应和更新。他聚焦的失败模式是"可塑性损失"——神经网络随着时间推移逐渐失去更新权重的能力，即使名义上仍处于训练状态。Javed 在阿尔伯塔大学的博士研究正是直接针对这一问题：如何让网络保持无限期学习的能力，同时不遗忘已有知识？

Oak Lab 成立的目的，就是构建永不停止学习的 AI 系统——能与环境持续交互、从经历中更新、并维持类似生物学习那种流动、持续智能的 agent。Sutton 认为，随着静态训练的局限性变得更加清晰，整个领域最终将被迫走向这一方向。他一贯的立场："我不奇怪。这个领域才奇怪。"
