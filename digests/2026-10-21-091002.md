**Aaron Levie**, CEO at Box

Levie published a detailed thesis on the emerging form factor of enterprise AI agents. Three pillars he identified: (1) improved tool and computer use enables agents to operate external data systems and build their own software; (2) models are achieving far better judgment in vertical domains — legal, bioresearch, security, supply chain; (3) the surrounding infrastructure (sandboxes, file systems, data pipelines) now supports longer-running, more complex agents. "You can almost imagine one or more of these systems for every role in the economy."

Box CEO Aaron Levie 发表了一篇详细论述企业级 AI agent 新兴形态的文章。他指出三大支柱：(1) 工具和计算机使用能力的提升，使 agent 能操控外部数据系统并自行构建所需软件；(2) 模型在法律、生物研究、安全、供应链等垂直领域的判断能力大幅提升；(3) 围绕 AI 的基础设施（沙箱、文件系统、数据管道等）已支持更长时间、更复杂的 agent 运行。"几乎可以想象经济体中的每个角色都配备一个或多个这样的系统。"
