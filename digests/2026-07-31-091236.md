### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "The Biggest AI Deployment Nobody Talks About | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas"

**The Takeaway:** The most consequential AI deployment in the world right now isn't in a data center or a consumer app — it's running on millions of trucks and vehicles covering 99% of U.S. roads every single day, and it just prevented 380,000 crashes last year.

Sanjit Biswas, co-founder and CEO of Samsara (NYSE: IOT, ~$20B market cap), has spent a decade doing the unglamorous work of digitizing the physical world — construction sites, electrical grids, long-haul trucking fleets, energy utilities. In a conversation with FirstMark Capital VC Matt Turck, Biswas argues that physical AI is the next frontier, and the opportunity is massive precisely because it's hard.

The challenge starts with data. Unlike the digital world — where decades of text and code were already machine-readable — physical operations generate messy, non-digitized information. Samsara's edge is years of building the sensors, cameras, and ingestion pipelines to convert that messiness into signal. Their customers generate 25 trillion data points per year. "These are not the tokens you're gonna find online," Biswas explains. "You can't crawl Reddit and find out about what happened on a construction site."

The shift Biswas describes is fundamental: AI has moved from IoT dashboards (show me the data) to agentic systems (take action for me) — scheduling maintenance, flagging safety hazards, coordinating frontline workers in real time. Samsara's safety AI alone helped prevent an estimated 380,000 road accidents last year.

One understated theme from the conversation: physical industries — transportation, construction, utilities — make up roughly 50% of global GDP, yet lag far behind the digital world in AI adoption. That gap is closing fast, especially as the energy grid build-out (tripling in five years what was built in the past 125 years) creates explosive demand for AI-assisted tradespeople. "There are just not enough electricians out there right now," Biswas observes. The opportunity for AI isn't to replace these workers — it's to make them dramatically more effective by eliminating the logistical overhead of their jobs.

### MAD Podcast — Matt Turck 主持 | "最大的 AI 部署，无人谈论 | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas"

**核心洞见：** 当今世界最重要的 AI 部署不在数据中心，也不在消费级应用里——它运行在数百万辆卡车和车辆上，每天覆盖美国 99% 的道路，仅去年就帮助预防了 38 万起交通事故。

Samsara（纽交所代码：IOT，市值约 200 亿美元）联合创始人兼 CEO Sanjit Biswas 花了十年时间做着并不光鲜的工作：将物理世界数字化——建筑工地、电力电网、长途货运车队、能源公用事业。在与 FirstMark Capital 投资人 Matt Turck 的对话中，Biswas 主张物理 AI 是下一个前沿领域，而其机会之所以巨大，恰恰因为它足够艰难。

挑战首先在于数据。与数字世界不同——后者积累了数十年的机器可读文本和代码——物理运营产生的是杂乱、非结构化的信息。Samsara 的护城河在于多年来构建的传感器、摄像头和数据摄取管道，将这种杂乱转化为有效信号。他们的客户每年产生 25 万亿个数据点。"这些 token 在网上根本找不到，"Biswas 解释道，"你没办法爬 Reddit 去了解一个建筑工地上发生了什么。"

Biswas 描述了一个根本性的转变：AI 已经从 IoT 仪表盘（给我看数据）演进为 agent 系统（替我采取行动）——自动安排维护、实时标记安全隐患、协调一线工人。仅 Samsara 的安全 AI，去年就帮助预防了约 38 万起道路事故。

对话中一个被低估的主题：交通、建筑、公用事业等实体产业约占全球 GDP 的 50%，却在 AI 采用上远落后于数字世界。这一差距正在迅速缩小，尤其是随着电网建设的爆发（未来五年新增量将是过去 125 年总量的三倍），对 AI 赋能技术工人的需求急剧攀升。"现在电工根本不够用，"Biswas 指出。AI 的机会不是取代这些工人，而是通过消除工作中的后勤负担，让他们的效率得到大幅提升。
