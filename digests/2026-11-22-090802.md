**No Priors — "Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg"**

The Takeaway: AI is finally positioned to do what decades of pharma couldn't — not by automating trial-and-error, but by replacing it with predictive models that simulate biology at experimental accuracy.

Max Jaderberg, President of Isomorphic Labs (backed by Alphabet), spent years at DeepMind working on AlphaGo and AlphaStar before co-founding Isomorphic in 2021 with the mission to solve all human disease using AI. His diagnosis of pharma's stagnation is sharp: drug development has remained empirically driven for a century, burning through expensive real-world iterations because we simply couldn't predict what would happen in patients before it did. That's not a cultural failure — it's a data and modeling problem.

The fix Isomorphic is building: simulation models that achieve "experimental accuracy" — meaning you can run the experiment in silico with the same confidence as running it in a lab. AlphaFold was the proof of concept. Now the goal is to extend that across the entire drug design stack: target identification, molecule design, toxicity prediction, and beyond. When those models hit experimental accuracy, Jaderberg argues, you stop iterating in the real world and only go there to validate — compressing the discovery phase dramatically.

One striking stat: rat models are only about 30% predictive of human clinical toxicity. Jaderberg thinks that benchmark is beatable — with in vitro assays, organoids, and AI-driven human tox models. "If one of my AI scientists came up to me and said, hey, we've got this amazing model — it's 30% predictive — you're like, okay, maybe do a bit better than that."

The company is internally focused on oncology, immunology, and inflammation, but their models are disease- and modality-agnostic. They partner with pharma companies on hard problems — including targets that have been worked on for 20+ years — using the Isomorphic drug design engine without licensing the model itself. Jaderberg's five-year ambition: a step-change in predictive disease biology and molecule precision, such that drug discovery timelines genuinely start to shrink.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IiKf3BgBezE

核心洞见：AI 终于有能力做到几十年制药行业未能实现的事——不是通过自动化"试错"，而是用能以实验精度模拟生物学的预测模型来取代它。

Isomorphic Labs（Alphabet 旗下）总裁 Max Jaderberg 在 DeepMind 参与了 AlphaGo 和 AlphaStar 的开发，于 2021 年联合创立 Isomorphic，目标是用 AI 解决所有人类疾病。他对制药行业停滞不前的诊断一针见血：一个世纪以来，药物开发始终是由经验驱动的，消耗在昂贵的真实世界迭代中，原因很简单——在药物进入患者体内之前，我们根本无法预测会发生什么。这不是文化失败，而是数据和建模的问题。

Isomorphic 正在构建的解决方案是：达到"实验精度"的仿真模型——即可以在计算机上运行实验，并获得与真实实验室相同的置信度。AlphaFold 是这一方向的概念验证。现在的目标是将其扩展到整个药物设计流程：靶点识别、分子设计、毒性预测等。一旦这些模型达到实验精度，Jaderberg 认为就不再需要在真实世界中迭代，只需去那里验证——大幅压缩药物发现阶段的时间。

一个令人震惊的数据：大鼠模型对人类临床毒性的预测准确率仅约 30%。Jaderberg 认为这个基准可以被超越——通过体外检测、类器官和 AI 驱动的人体毒性模型。"如果我的一位 AI 科学家来告诉我，嘿，我们有一个很棒的模型，它有 30% 的预测准确率——你会说，好吧，也许可以做得更好一点。"

该公司内部聚焦于肿瘤学、免疫学和炎症领域，但其模型对疾病类型和药物形式保持不可知论。他们与制药公司在难题上展开合作——包括已被研究超过 20 年的靶点——使用 Isomorphic 药物设计引擎，但不对外授权模型本身。Jaderberg 的五年愿景：在预测疾病生物学和分子精确性上实现跨越式进步，使药物发现的时间线真正开始缩短。
