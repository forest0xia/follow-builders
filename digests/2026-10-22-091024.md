**AI & I by Every — "How a Frontier Team Can Help Your Company Keep Up With AI"**

**The Takeaway:** The companies winning with AI aren't the ones with the best models — they're the ones with a dedicated "frontier team" that separates exploration from execution.

Every, the AI-focused media and software company, convened its entire frontier team to share what they've each been building and learning. CEO Dan Shipper frames the core dilemma every company faces: either ignore the frontier and risk being disrupted by "some agent-native version of your product that you didn't see coming," or chase every new model and build nothing lasting. The solution: a small group of explorers whose job is to stay at the bleeding edge so everyone else doesn't have to.

Two archetypes define the best frontier teams: **Pirates** (move fast, try everything, accept the mess) and **Architects** (compress that mess into reliable, maintainable systems). The handoff between them is where good ideas actually become products.

The team's actual output is striking: Head of Evals Mike Taylor used Claude Opus with UltraCode to pull together an entire explainer video from Slack messages and meeting notes — "one model can't read all that context, so it sent hundreds of agents, one per meeting." Senior editor Jack Chang built his own tabless browser to protect his attention ("now the models are so good I can just build my own"). Writer Katie Parrott is adding an `audience.md` file to her compound writing plugin to help the AI understand who it's writing for. Engineer Yannick Jung built a multiplayer AI orchestrator that breaks projects into plans and pings him only when decisions are needed. And compound engineer Kieran Klassen is now building a full video editor and writing tool — work that simply wasn't possible a year ago.

Frontier Team Wrangler Arielle Shipper captures the broader shift: *"As these models get smarter and smarter, my ability to actually build the capacity for that grows. It's not a power I've had before, and it feels magical."*

Mike Taylor's cheat sheet for using models today: stop being hesitant about giving AI full context. "Give all your context over to AI… just make that leap." And then protect other people's attention by making your AI-produced work more digestible before you share it.

**AI & I by Every — "企业如何用前沿团队跟上AI发展步伐"**

**核心要点：** 在AI竞争中胜出的公司，不是拥有最好模型的公司——而是拥有专属"前沿团队"、将探索与执行分离的公司。

AI聚焦媒体和软件公司Every召集了整个前沿团队，分享他们各自在构建和学习的内容。CEO Dan Shipper描述了每家公司面临的核心困境：要么忽视前沿发展，冒着被"你没预见到的agent原生版产品"颠覆的风险；要么追逐每个新模型，最终什么都建不成。解决方案：一个小型探索团队，专职保持在技术最前沿，让其他人不必时刻分心。

最好的前沿团队由两种原型构成：**海盗型**（快速行动、什么都尝试、接受混乱）和**架构师型**（将混乱压缩成可靠、可维护的系统）。两者之间的交接，正是好想法变成真正产品的地方。

团队的实际产出令人印象深刻：评测负责人Mike Taylor用配备UltraCode的Claude Opus，从Slack消息和会议记录中整合出完整的解说视频——"一个模型无法读完所有上下文，于是它派出了数百个agent，每次会议一个"。高级编辑Jack Chang为保护注意力构建了自己的无标签浏览器（"现在模型已经足够强大，我可以直接自己造了"）。写作者Katie Parrott在她的写作插件中新增了`audience.md`文件，帮助AI理解受众是谁。工程师Yannick Jung构建了一个多人协作AI协调器，将项目拆解成计划，只在需要决策时才提醒他。复合工程师Kieran Klassen正在构建完整的视频编辑器和写作工具——这些工作在一年前根本不可能完成。

前沿团队"驯兽师"Arielle Shipper道出了更广泛的变革：*"随着模型越来越强大，我实际上构建相应能力的能力也在增长。这是我以前从未有过的力量，感觉很神奇。"*

评测负责人Mike Taylor今日使用模型的秘诀：不要再犹豫，把所有上下文都给AI。"把所有上下文都交给AI……直接迈出那一步。"然后，通过让你的AI产出更易于理解来保护他人的注意力，再分享给他们。
