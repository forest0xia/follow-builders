### Training Data: "Chai Discovery's Bitter Lesson: Drug Design Is Another Scaling Problem"

**The Takeaway:** Drug design is just another scaling problem — and Chai Discovery is betting that the bitter lesson that transformed AI (more data, more compute, simpler models) will eventually cure diseases that have eluded medicine for decades.

Josh and Matt, co-founders of Chai Discovery, spent years in the field before founding Chai: Matt did his PhD on protein structure prediction and was among the first to apply deep learning to the problem; Josh was on the early OpenAI team (GPT-1, GPT-2, scaling laws), co-authored ESM (a foundational language model for protein sequences), and has been asking since then: "If models can learn English and German, why can't they learn DNA and protein?" The name Chai stands for Chemistry and AI.

The numbers frame the scale of the bet. When Chai started in 2024, the state of the art for antibody design was a 0.1% binding success rate — one in a thousand designed molecules would actually bind in the lab. Chai-2 pushed that to 15%. Now Chai can zero-shot molecules that pharma partners are considering bringing to clinical trials. Partners include Eli Lilly, Novartis, Argenx, and Pfizer.

The core guiding principle is simplicity — what they call being "bitter lesson pilled." Chai-1 had 23 distinct submodules, which made research iteration painfully slow. Rather than add more modules to unlock harder targets, they strip back to find what's truly fundamental, identify their scaling laws, and trust that better models will generalize. As Matt puts it: "One of our big guiding principles is just simplicity... once you have that, the whole research process and identifying these types of scaling directions becomes a lot simpler."

Data compounds from two sources: the protein databank (structural data accumulated since 1970) and biological sequence databases that may hold more tokens than the entire English-language internet. As models improve, they predict better structures, which feed the next generation — a flywheel similar to what RLHF did for LLMs once the base model was strong enough.

The business model is infrastructure, not drugs. Chai enables pharma companies rather than competing with them for pipeline ownership — a deliberate bet on staying focused on model quality. Better models win more partnerships; more partnership data improves models further. "If you look at our partners, Eli Lilly, Novartis, Argenx, Pfizer — these are not companies that are going to take things for granted. You have to really deliver."

**核心要点：** 药物设计不过是另一个规模化问题——Chai Discovery 正押注于这样一条信念：改变了 AI 领域的苦涩教训（更多数据、更多算力、更简洁的模型），终将攻克那些困扰医学几十年的疑难疾病。

Josh 和 Matt 是 Chai Discovery 的联合创始人，两人在创业前都在这个领域深耕多年：Matt 的博士研究聚焦于蛋白质结构预测，是最早将深度学习应用于该问题的人之一；Josh 曾是 OpenAI 早期团队成员（参与 GPT-1、GPT-2、规模化定律），并共同创作了 ESM（蛋白质序列语言模型的奠基性工作），此后一直在追问：「如果模型能学会英语和德语，为什么不能学会 DNA 和蛋白质语言？」Chai 这个名字代表 Chemistry and AI。

一组数字勾勒出这场押注的规模。Chai 在 2024 年成立时，抗体设计的最高水平结合成功率仅为 0.1%——每一千个设计的分子中只有一个真正能在实验室中结合。Chai-2 将这一比率提升至 15%。如今，Chai 已能零样本设计出制药合作伙伴考虑推进临床试验的分子。合作方包括礼来、诺华、Argenx 和辉瑞。

核心指导原则是简洁——他们称之为「苦涩教训式思维」。Chai-1 拥有 23 个子模块，这让研究迭代极为缓慢。他们的选择不是为解锁更难的靶点而叠加更多模块，而是剥离到最根本的东西，找出规模化定律，相信更好的模型自然会泛化。Matt 如此表述：「我们最核心的指导原则之一就是简洁……一旦找到了，整个研究过程和规模化方向的识别就会简单得多。」

数据从两个来源复利积累：蛋白质数据库（1970 年以来积累的结构数据）和生物序列数据库（其 token 总量可能超过整个英语互联网）。随着模型改进，它们预测出更好的结构，进而成为下一代模型的训练数据——这个飞轮与 RLHF 在基础模型足够强大后对 LLM 的作用如出一辙。

商业模式是基础设施，而非药物。Chai 为制药公司赋能，而非与其争夺管线所有权——这是专注于模型质量的深思熟虑之选。更好的模型赢得更多合作，更多合作数据进一步改进模型。「看看我们的合作伙伴——礼来、诺华、Argenx、辉瑞——这些公司不会对任何事情想当然。你必须真正兑现承诺。」
