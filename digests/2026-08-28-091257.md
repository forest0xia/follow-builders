### Aaron Levie — CEO, Box

Box CEO Aaron Levie delivered a sweeping analysis on the heels of strong Q2 results: revenue of $321.1M, up 9% (11% in constant currency — the highest constant-currency growth in 14 quarters), with full-year guidance raised to $1.29B. But the more interesting part of his post was the enterprise AI diagnosis behind those numbers.

Levie argues that the most advanced AI is only as useful as the enterprise knowledge it can access — and most of that knowledge lives in unstructured content: contracts, financial documents, marketing assets, product roadmaps. The companies winning the next wave won't just swap in better models; they'll need applied AI platforms that can securely bring any model to any workflow, with the ability to swap providers on the fly as capabilities evolve. And with the OpenAI/Hugging Face incident still fresh, he underscored the non-negotiables: robust guardrails, comprehensive audit logs, and real-time security alerts.

### Claude Blog: Claude in Chrome is Generally Available

Claude in Chrome — Anthropic's extension that lets Claude operate inside your own browser — is now generally available on every paid Claude plan. The announcement comes with a significant new capability: Claude can now take actions autonomously in the browser without requiring approval for every single step. A safety classifier validates each action before execution to ensure it aligns with the user's intent and is safe to perform.

The rollout follows an extended pilot period, during which Anthropic hardened the system's defenses against prompt injection — the attack vector where malicious instructions hidden in web content (pages, emails, form fields) attempt to redirect an AI agent. Since the pilot, Anthropic improved model training, upgraded its probes, and added a new layer of classifiers to catch injection attempts before they can influence Claude's behavior. Claude in Chrome lets Claude interact with any website — including internal dashboards, legacy systems, and vendor portals that don't have native connectors — using the user's existing logins.
https://claude.com/blog/claude-in-chrome-generally-available

Chrome 版 Claude——Anthropic 推出的让 Claude 在用户浏览器中运行的扩展程序——现已向所有付费 Claude 计划全面开放。此次发布还带来了一项重要的新能力：Claude 现在可以自主在浏览器中执行操作，无需对每个步骤逐一确认。每项操作在执行前都会经过安全分类器验证，确保其符合用户意图且安全可靠。

此次全面发布是在经历了较长时间的公测期后推出的，期间 Anthropic 持续强化了系统对提示注入攻击的防御能力——这种攻击方式将恶意指令隐藏在网页内容、邮件或表单字段中，试图劫持 AI agent 的行为。自公测以来，Anthropic 改进了模型训练、升级了探针，并新增了一层分类器，在注入指令影响 Claude 行为之前将其拦截。Chrome 版 Claude 可让 Claude 访问任意网站——包括内部仪表板、遗留系统和供应商门户，即使这些网站没有原生连接器——并使用用户现有的登录状态进行操作。
https://claude.com/blog/claude-in-chrome-generally-available

### Claude Blog: Claude Gets Its Own Browser in Cowork

Anthropic has added a built-in browser directly inside Claude Cowork on the desktop app. When a task requires web access, a browser opens in the side panel — Claude navigates pages, reads content, clicks links, fills forms, and completes the job. No extension needed, nothing shared from your personal browser unless you explicitly choose to.

The built-in browser is Claude's browser, not yours: it's completely separate from your tabs, bookmarks, and passwords. You can bring over specific site logins from Chrome, Edge, or Firefox (excluding banking, email, and SSO sites by default). This distinguishes it from Claude in Chrome — the built-in browser is for handing off web tasks entirely (background research, collecting invoices from vendor portals), while Claude in Chrome is for acting on pages you already have open. If you use Claude in Chrome today, it stays your default. Rollout begins this week for Pro, Max, and Team plans in the desktop app; Enterprise admins can enable it for their org now.
https://claude.com/blog/cowork-built-in-browser

Anthropic 在桌面版 Claude Cowork 中直接内置了一个浏览器。当任务需要访问网络时，浏览器会在侧边栏中打开——Claude 负责页面导航、内容阅读、点击链接、填写表单并完成任务。无需安装扩展，除非用户主动选择，否则个人浏览器的内容不会被共享。

内置浏览器是 Claude 自己的浏览器，与用户的浏览器完全分离：Claude 无法看到用户的标签页、书签和密码。用户可以从 Chrome、Edge 或 Firefox 中将特定网站的登录信息迁移过来（银行、邮件和 SSO 网站默认排除在外）。这与 Chrome 版 Claude 的定位有所区别——内置浏览器适合将网页任务完全交给 Claude（如后台调研、从供应商门户收集发票），而 Chrome 版 Claude 则适合在用户已打开的页面上进行操作。如果你目前使用 Chrome 版 Claude，它仍将保持默认。Pro、Max 和 Team 计划的桌面版用户本周开始陆续收到更新；企业管理员即日起可为其组织开启该功能。
https://claude.com/blog/cowork-built-in-browser

### AI & I by Every: "A $10B Hedge Fund's AI Playbook (Best of the Pod)"

**The Takeaway:** A hedge fund CEO who went from quant trader to full AI-first transformation is the most credible proof that "AI-first" can mean something real — not a memo, but a cultural operating system enforced from the top.

Dan Shipper at Every sat down with Will, CEO/CIO/Managing Partner of Walleye Capital, a ~$10B AUM hedge fund, for one of the most practically grounded AI implementation conversations to come out of the financial world. Will is a rare find: a bonafide engineer (Princeton, Oxford math PhD) who started his career writing code for algorithmic strategies, and who is now running one of the more aggressive AI transformations in institutional finance.

The conversation opened with Will reading from his internal memo — the most quotable moment of the episode: *"Using ChatGPT is not cheating. That's a non-applicable idea from academia. I use ChatGPT to write this email. You should be using it too and be proud of it. As a hedge fund, we should be ashamed to leave money on the table by ignoring tools that make us faster, smarter, and more effective. From the very top, we are building a culture around AI. Not using these tools is like refusing to use the Internet in 1995 because it wasn't perfect."*

What makes Will's approach unusual isn't the memo — it's that he has the technical background, conviction, and organizational authority to back it up. He was deliberate about going public: he cited three audiences — current Walleye employees, future hires, and ecosystem partners — as the reasons for doing the podcast at all. This isn't performative AI-washing; it's a CEO who believes the next competitive advantage in finance is AI capability, and is willing to be publicly accountable for building it.
