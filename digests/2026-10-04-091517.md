**The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer | Renen Hallak, VAST Data"**

**The Takeaway:** The most important layer in the AI stack — the software infrastructure that feeds data to GPUs — is being built by one company almost nobody has heard of, and it's already valued at $30 billion.

Renen Hallak founded VAST Data in 2016, two years before the Transformer paper and three years before ChatGPT. His bet: that building AI at scale would require a completely new storage architecture — not just faster or bigger, but a fundamentally different design. The old approach, "shared nothing," breaks down past a certain scale because nodes have to constantly communicate with each other. VAST flipped the model: put storage on the other side of the network and let all nodes share access to all data simultaneously. One customer today runs a single cluster of multiple exabytes delivering tens of terabytes per second.

Hallak's framing of the AI stack is clarifying: VAST sits in the "software infrastructure" layer — Jensen Huang's five-layer cake — between hardware and models. And he argues that layer is where the most durable value accumulates. "If you look at the most valuable companies in the world today — Amazon, Microsoft, Apple, Google — those are the companies that built those software layers of the cloud, of the PC era, of the mobile era, of the Internet."

The demand picture is staggering. One AI cloud customer recently told VAST they'd need 500 petabytes over three years. A week later, they came back asking for an extra 2 exabytes on top. "My expectation is that they'll come back again in a quarter or two and say we need a double digit number of exabytes — we undershot."

VAST's newest move is "data enclave" — confidential computing that lets enterprises run inference on-premises without exposing their proprietary data to model providers, while also ensuring model weights remain encrypted and never exposed to the enterprise. NVIDIA, Cisco, and Super Micro are among the launch partners. The goal: unlock the massive regulated-industry market that currently can't use commercial AI models at all.

Hallak on building speed: "You find the limiting factor and you get rid of it. Then you find the new limiting factor and you get rid of it."

**MAD Podcast（Matt Turck 主持）——"谁在给 GPU 喂数据？揭秘 AI 隐形 $300 亿基础层 | VAST Data CEO Renen Hallak"**

**核心观点：** AI 技术栈中最关键的一层——负责向 GPU 输送数据的软件基础设施——正由一家几乎无人知晓的公司构建，而它的估值已达 300 亿美元。

Renen Hallak 于 2016 年创立了 VAST Data，那是 Transformer 论文发布前两年，ChatGPT 问世前三年。他的判断是：大规模构建 AI 需要一种全新的存储架构——不只是更快或更大，而是从根本上不同的设计。传统的"无共享"架构在超过一定规模后会崩溃，因为节点之间的通信会呈指数级增长。VAST 反转了这一模型：将存储置于网络另一侧，让所有节点同时共享所有数据的访问权限。目前某客户运行着一个多 EB 级的单一集群，提供每秒数十 TB 的吞吐量。

Hallak 对 AI 技术栈的解读极具启发性：VAST 处于黄仁勋所描述的五层蛋糕中的"软件基础设施"层——介于硬件与模型之间。他认为，这一层才是最持久价值积累的地方。"放眼当今全球最有价值的公司——Amazon、Microsoft、Apple、Google——它们都是那些构建了云时代、PC 时代、移动时代、互联网时代软件层的公司。"

需求端的数字令人瞠目结舌。某 AI cloud 客户近期告知 VAST，未来三年需要约 500 PB。一周后，他们又回来说还需要额外 2 EB。"我预计他们再过一两个季度还会回来说需要两位数 EB 级别的容量——他们低估了。"

VAST 的最新动作是"数据飞地"——一种机密计算方案，让企业能够在本地运行推理，既不向模型提供商暴露专有数据，同时也确保模型权重经过加密，不会被企业访问。NVIDIA、Cisco 和 Super Micro 是发布合作伙伴。目标是打开目前完全无法使用商业 AI 模型的庞大监管行业市场。

Hallak 谈构建速度："找到限制因素，消除它。然后找到新的限制因素，再消除它。"
